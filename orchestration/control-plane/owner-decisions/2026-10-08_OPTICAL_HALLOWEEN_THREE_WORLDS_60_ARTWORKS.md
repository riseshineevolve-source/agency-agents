## New scope and creative authorization
Owner approved all **three winning concept directions** rather than choosing one. Develop three self-contained, tightly connected 20-theme illustration collections (60 hero art candidates overall). They are related as premium monochrome optical art but have distinct stories, architecture, optics, emotional journeys, camera grammars and end reveals:
1. **The House That Remembers** — a house that preserves an interrupted welcome and finally opens.
2. **The Midnight Carnival Below the City** — a stalled underground performance whose missing beat is restored through collective participation.
3. **The Eclipse Museum of Lost Nights** — future-oriented reflections of nights not yet lived, where choice releases one protected possibility.

### Story and visual requirement
All **60 scenes must contribute causally** to one of three 5-act stories. Each collection plants visual evidence early and pays it off in its final image. Avoid isolated filler and derivative duplicates. For each scene, create 3–5 truly distinct concepts, have Visual Storyteller rank them, Brand Guardian reject generic kitsch, then generate selected artwork, test anatomy/perspective/reflection, repair only concrete faults, and pass print-scale visual gate before promotion.
Mandatory squad: Visual Storyteller lead, Brand Guardian, Image Prompt Engineer, Reality Checker, UI/Print Finish-Gate Reviewer. Additional only for meaningful value; reviewer judgements require actual evidence, not pretend independent agents.

### Creative standards
World-class high-art optical illusion in ink black/white, 8.5×11 portrait planning; exceptional unconventional perspective, physically meaningful mirrors, controlled paradoxes, cast-shadow silhouettes, tessellation following physical structures, hidden second visual read, strong black anchors and broad useable white coloring regions. Optical Animals serves only as visual DNA reference, **not** composition, animal subject or production source to copy. No mundane coloring-page aesthetic, repeated swirl tunnels, mandala/sticker/stock Halloween, kawaii/chibi, fuzzy fur, unphysical stray repeated faces, cramped micro-lines or uncontrolled grayscale gradients.

### Strict deferred work
This lane **must not** build seek-and-find, exact tokens/masks, answer maps, pagination, book assembly, KDP production, visual factory integration or edit Optical Animals production assets. Reusable factory integration waits until Optical Animals proves its own pipeline and receives a separate owner authorization.
