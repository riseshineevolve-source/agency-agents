## 2. Creative contract for all thirty routes

- **World first:** approximately 70% of the visual argument should come from optical geometry embodied in door returns, reliefs, drawers, canopy tension, tile laps, stove fittings, piano boards, bed supports, stairs, or table/piece construction. This is an editorial composition target, not measured image coverage. A large prop against patterned wallpaper fails it.
- **Binary material:** only `#000000` and `#FFFFFF` in the eventual image. No gray pixels, graphite, gray glass, hatch tone, photographic texture, or soft shading. All artworks are 8.5 × 11 portrait, image-only, without lettering, annotation, border, or infographic elements.
- **Broad intervals:** use a few thick structural sweeps with large white intervals between them. Principal white channels should target at least about 0.2 inch at intended size, with several hero regions about 0.5 inch or wider. These are unmeasured design intentions. Fine detail never carries the decisive clue.
- **Black anchors:** propose roughly 40–55% solid-black coverage, adjusted for silhouette and white usability. White fabric is still opaque fabric; white glass is not automatically a reflecting surface. Material identity comes from construction and boundaries.
- **One optical cause:** a route changes the camera and physical design around the canonical mechanism, not the incident. Each image has one governing illusion; only H-01's observation-dependent aperture and H-07's localized seam need a displayed fantasy exception in this batch. Ordinary bowed fittings may be sculptural but remain solid and Euclidean. Never add a second magical law to intensify a route.
- **Two readings:** the thumbnail gives an alarming architectural mass; close inspection gives its actual cause and next action. The second reading must be physically supported, not a hidden token, symbolic face, extra actor, or early explanation of the host's intention.
- **Actor restraint:** Mara remains an adult in her canonical straight coat, trousers, boots, swept-back hair, and notched left cuff. Keep the V01 hand assignments: bare left hand at H-01; bare right hand retrieves the left glove at H-03; left glove worn from H-04 onward; right hand lifts/carries the cup; gloved left hand approaches the rook in H-10. The cat appears only in H-02/H-08 within this batch and stays small and anatomically ordinary.
- **Do not merge routes:** A/B/C below are new proposals, not the old source letters. Their cameras and builds are alternatives. There are thirty candidates, not thirty simultaneous additions to the house.

“O” below means the canonical selected MASTER/V01 direction, not an unseen original moodboard. Its original selection letters are recorded to prevent confusion. Narrative incidents and the H-11–H-20 payoffs remain authoritative. Distinctness is specified and audited at concept level; originality and successful optical impact cannot be guaranteed for a future generated image by prose alone.
