## Job
Solve the friction gap:
"What happens when the puzzle gets hard?"

## Buyer situation
Parent wants challenge, but not a book that becomes unusable after one difficult case.

## Hook
**STUCK? DON'T OPEN THE ANSWER YET.**

## Verified proof
3-Level Hint Vault:
- Level 1: what to notice;
- Level 2: where to focus;
- Level 3: stronger nudge;
- full reasoning solution remains separate.

## Carousel — 5 slides

1. **STUCK IS PART OF DETECTIVE WORK.**
2. **LEVEL 1 — WHAT TO NOTICE**
3. **LEVEL 2 — WHERE TO FOCUS**
4. **LEVEL 3 — STRONGER NUDGE**
5. **THE FULL SOLUTION CAN WAIT.**

CTA:
**SAVE THIS FOR THE KID WHO HATES BEING TOLD THE ANSWER TOO SOON.**

## Reel / Short — 9–12s

Visual:
real case page -> Level 1 -> back to case -> Level 2 -> back to case -> Level 3 -> separate Solution Files indicator.

VO:
"Getting stuck does not mean the case is over. Start with one small nudge, go back to the evidence, and take a stronger hint only if you need it. The full reasoning is still there when you want it."

CTA:
**SEE HOW THE HINT VAULT WORKS.**

## Instagram caption

Sometimes the fastest way to ruin a good puzzle is to reveal the answer too soon.

The Detective Academy Hint Vault gives the reader three levels of support before the separate Solution Files:

Level 1: what to notice.
Level 2: where to focus.
Level 3: a stronger nudge.

Take the smallest useful hint.
Go back to the case.
Keep investigating.

CTA:
**Save this for launch.**

**Save this for the mystery kid who likes figuring things out.**
