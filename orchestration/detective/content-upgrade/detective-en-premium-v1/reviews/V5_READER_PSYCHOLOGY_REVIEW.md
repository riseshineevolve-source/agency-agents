# Independent Review — Reader Psychology / Ages 8–12

Reviewer lens: Psychologist
Baseline: V4
Status: REVIEW ONLY

## Strengths

- Reader has genuine agency through solving.
- Humor lowers intimidation.
- Case Wall creates memory ownership.
- Delayed callbacks reward attention.
- Final certification pays off persistence rather than "being special."

## FIX

### P1 — Belonging should be earned through repeated participation
Best mechanism: team behavior changes around the reader.
Avoid repeated welcome/praise.

### P2 — Give each deduction an immediate purpose
8–12 readers tolerate complexity when they know why the next move matters.
"Find the companion" must always connect to a concrete question.

### P3 — Protect competence
Instructions should be clear without sounding remedial.
Training Case 01 can be simple if framed as learning the Academy method, not as "easy for beginners."

### P4 — Vary cognitive/emotional rhythm
After dense map logic, give a visual, physical or humorous case.
After a funny case, allow a quieter suspense beat.
Avoid 5 identical "read map -> find witness" emotional experiences in a row.

### P5 — Mystery tension should feel safe
Strangeness is compelling; threat inflation is unnecessary.
Use wrong records, old systems, missing context and unexplained callbacks.

### P6 — The payoff must acknowledge remembered work
The finale should explicitly make the reader feel:
"I did not guess this. I built this answer over the whole book."
