# MEET THE HAPPY MAKERS

They are not a perfectly calm Christmas family.

They lose things, run late, change plans, laugh at the wrong moment, and somehow still make ordinary December nights worth remembering.

## NINI, 4
### The Pocket-Sized Joy Distributor

The smallest Happy Maker and, according to Nini, a fully qualified decision-maker.

She dances to almost anything with a beat, including kitchen appliances, "reads" books with complete confidence whether or not anyone has taught her all the words yet, and gives hugs with enough commitment to interrupt most adult plans.

She is also extremely good at noticing when everybody else is taking something far too seriously.

**Life motto:** If everyone is sitting quietly, something probably needs improving.

---

## ALIO, 7
### The Chief Chaos Engineer

Alio runs on curiosity, snacks, and roughly 120% renewable energy.

He builds enormous block cities, invents games halfway through playing other games, bounces on anything technically designed for bouncing, and can become a Kung Fu master with breathtaking intensity for approximately twelve seconds.

Some of his ideas are excellent.

The others are usually excellent stories later.

**Life motto:** Maximum fun. Mostly safe.

---

## DILO, 9
### The Striker & Systems Department

Part footballer, part gamer, part unofficial quality-control officer.

Dilo likes rules, scores, tech, timing, and knowing whether something officially counts. He can discuss a disputed point with the seriousness of a video replay review and still be the first person to notice when someone quietly needs help.

His hair also appears to have its own compliance standards.

**Life motto:** Check the rules. Then improve the game.

---

## LULI, 12
### The Elegant Detective

The oldest sibling, which means she has had the longest to observe the evidence.

Luli loves mysteries, aerial silks, language, and details everybody else somehow walked past. She can solve a small household mystery faster than most adults can find their keys, then deliver the answer in one sentence while everyone else is still explaining the problem.

She is stylish, precise, quietly funny, and not easily impressed by unnecessary drama.

**Life motto:** There is usually a clue. Look properly.

---

## MIMI
### The Family Control Center

Mimi can be thinking about dinner, tomorrow's schedule, a missing glove, a message she still needs to answer, and whether anyone has seen the tape at exactly the same time.

Peaceful? Not especially.

Effective? Suspiciously often.

She is warm, quick on her feet, permanently aware of the practical details, and still occasionally acts as the family's self-appointed Fashion Police when somebody attempts a particularly ambitious outfit.

In December, Mimi does not exactly control the chaos.

She gives it somewhere to put its coat.

**Life motto:** We can sort it out. Probably after tea.
