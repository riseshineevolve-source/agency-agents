## Remaining findings

The fresh native-English pass found a small set of bounded issues. They are not redesigns.

### F1 — MUST FIX — D21.PAUSE native English

Current phrase:
`This is only a short less, not perfect quiet.`

Problem:
This is not idiomatic native English. It reads like a successful idea carried too literally from another language.

Required direction:
Use a simple native formulation such as:
`This is just a little less noise for one minute, not perfect silence.`

Impact:
low scope / definite native-language defect.

---

### F2 — MUST FIX — D24.PAUSE native English

Current:
`think of one person or family outside this room you would like to wish something good for today.`

Problem:
`wish something good for` is grammatically understandable but not the way a native English family book would normally phrase this.

Required direction:
Use a natural construction such as:
`think of one person or family outside this room you'd like to send a good wish to today.`

Impact:
low scope / definite native-language defect.

---

### F3 — MUST FIX — D19.PLAY actor clarity

START names the first observer, then PLAY says:
`The guesser turns around or closes their eyes.`

Problem:
The reader has to infer that the starter is the first guesser.

Required direction:
Make it explicit:
`That person is the first guesser.`

No mechanic changes.

---

### F4 — MUST FIX — D14.PAUSE spatial / consent clarity

Current sequence gives no seating arrangement, then says:
`the shoulder of the person to your right`.

Problem:
"to your right" depends on an arrangement the activity never established.

Required direction:
Use arrangement-free wording:
`someone beside you`, with the existing explicit consent retained.

This is especially better for two people.

---

### F5 — SHOULD FIX — D18.PLAY odd-number setup

Current:
`Pair up.`
Later:
`ODD GROUP: In a group of three...`

Problem:
For five players, the family can infer that the leftover person joins a pair, but premium instructions should not require that inference.

Recommended micro-fix:
Immediately after `Pair up`:
`If there is an odd number of players, one group will have three.`

The existing three-person rotation then works unchanged.

---

### F6 — SHOULD FIX — D20.PAUSE idiom

Current:
`Make two light fists and count slowly to three.`

Problem:
`light fists` is understandable but not idiomatic.

Recommended:
`Lightly clench both hands and count slowly to three.`

No function change.

---

### F7 — SHOULD FIX — D15.PAUSE native metaphor

Current:
`Backpacks, bags, groceries, and the entire day can stay in your shoulders...`

Problem:
The image works, but the English wording feels slightly translated.

Recommended direction:
Keep the same idea, but make it native:
`Backpacks, bags, groceries, and the whole day can leave your shoulders tense long after the coats are hanging up.`

The following line about December near the ears may remain if it still reads naturally after this repair.

---

### F8 — SMALL NATIVE POLISH — D14.PLAY

Current category example:
`something you would bring on a free day`

Problem:
`a free day` is understandable but not the most natural US/UK family phrasing.

Recommended:
`something you'd bring on a day with no plans`
or another equally neutral native phrase.

---

### F9 — SMALL NATIVE POLISH — D09.BETWEEN_US

Current example fragment:
`Loaned out a charger.`

Problem:
For this family context, `lent you a charger` is more natural in both US and UK English.

Recommended:
`Lent you a charger.`

---

### F10 — SMALL NATIVE POLISH — D04.PAUSE

Current:
`a lamp, tree light, or a candle...`

Problem:
Singular `tree light` is slightly awkward.

Recommended:
`a lamp, one Christmas-tree light, or a candle...`
or
`a lamp, a light on the tree, or a candle...`

Keep adult candle safety wording.

---

### F11 — SMALL AGE-FIT POLISH — D20.PLAY

Current:
`Invent one new category that can be checked objectively afterward.`

Problem:
Correct English, but `objectively` is more adult/workshop-like than the surrounding 8–12 family voice.

Recommended:
`Invent one new category with a clear answer you can check afterward.`
