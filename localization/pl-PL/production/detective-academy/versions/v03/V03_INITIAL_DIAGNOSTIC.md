# V03 Initial Full-Book Diagnostic — Why V02 Failed the Owner Read

Status: diagnostic, not a rewrite.

## Book-wide defects

### D01 — English sentence skeleton under Polish words
Severity: BLOCK for reader-facing approval.
Pattern: clauses and emphasis follow English cadence, producing Polish that is grammatically acceptable but recognizably translated.

### D02 — Too much interface/system register in narrative
Severity: HIGH.
Some phrases read like localized UI labels or process documentation rather than a Polish adventure book.

### D03 — Dialogue keeps source joke shape instead of rebuilding the joke
Severity: HIGH.
The literal semantic joke often survives while comic timing does not.

### D04 — Happy Makers voices are not Polish-distinct enough
Severity: HIGH.
Characters remain mechanically distinct in content, but sentence rhythm does not always make them sound like six different Polish speakers.

### D05 — Repetitive spatial-solution phrasing
Severity: MEDIUM.
Technically exact phrases such as repeated “znajduje się / zajmuje / trafia” create a solver-report cadence. Solutions need variation without weakening exactness.

### D06 — Headings sometimes sound translated, not authored
Severity: MEDIUM/HIGH.
Case titles and section headers should be judged as Polish publishing copy, not lexical equivalence.

### D07 — Cultural localization underused
Severity: HIGH.
Where the source has no mechanic/visual dependency, wording should use natural Polish family/school/adventure language rather than preserving Anglophone phrasing.

### D08 — Over-fragmentation
Severity: MEDIUM.
English graphic-novel fragments were sometimes carried into Polish even where Polish needs a complete sentence or a different cut point.

### D09 — Exposition disguised as dialogue
Severity: MEDIUM.
Some lines explain mechanics in a voice no Polish child/relative would naturally use.

### D10 — Humor density is uneven
Severity: MEDIUM.
Some English jokes translate weakly while other pages become too serious. Rebuild humor by character and situation.

## What did NOT fail
- puzzle truth;
- overall case architecture;
- codename system after FINAL70 correction;
- Hint Vault structure;
- most factual continuity;
- Room Zero macro-payoff.

Therefore V03 is a **language/culture/voice re-authoring**, not a new plot or puzzle design.
