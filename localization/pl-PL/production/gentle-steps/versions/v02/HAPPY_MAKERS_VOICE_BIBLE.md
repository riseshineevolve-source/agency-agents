# Happy Makers Voice Bible for Gentle Steps PL Version 02

Status: WORKING VOICE LOCK FOR V02

This file defines comic mechanisms and editorial roles, not new biography.

## Dilo

Core mechanism: dry logic, systems, rules, mock quality control, understated technology language.

Works well when:
- a game has rules, sequence, counting or error states;
- something absurd can be treated as a serious system problem;
- he notices loopholes or "technical" failure.

Avoid:
- generic cheerleading;
- meme slang;
- turning every line into a tech joke.

Target feel:
smart, dry, slightly suspicious of chaos, but clearly enjoying it.

## Luli

Core mechanism: precision, practical older-sibling energy, elegant deadpan, low tolerance for vague answers.

Works well when:
- instructions need one sharp boundary;
- a conversation prompt risks becoming grand or sentimental;
- a small truth needs more weight than a speech.

Avoid:
- sounding like a teacher;
- correcting everyone constantly;
- corporate precision.

Target feel:
quick, exact, warm underneath the dry delivery.

## Alio

Core mechanism: movement, curiosity, self-incriminating observations, enthusiastic literal thinking.

Works well when:
- the activity has physical comedy;
- a rule can be taken one step further;
- he can admit he is probably the reason a warning exists.

Avoid:
- babyish cuteness;
- random noise for its own sake.

Target feel:
energy plus intelligence, not "the little kid".

## Nini

Core mechanism: mischievous permission, sideways observation, comic exaggeration.

Works well when:
- an activity could feel awkward;
- the family needs permission not to do it perfectly;
- a small act can be reframed as an event.

Avoid:
- forced sass;
- sarcasm at another family member's expense.

Target feel:
playful and quick, never mean.

## Mimi

Core mechanism: warm domestic observation, practical body-awareness, calm deadpan.

Works well when:
- a quiet section could become wellness language;
- the joke comes from ordinary comfort, food, blankets or the tired body;
- the page needs warmth without sentimentality.

Avoid:
- maternal lecturing;
- syrupy reassurance.

Target feel:
grounded, observant, gently funny.

## Collective Happy Makers voice

Use only when the book needs a shared closing thought.

It should:
- land the emotional point simply;
- avoid summarizing the lesson;
- never sound like a motivational poster;
- allow a little warmth after the joke rather than adding another joke.

## V02 humor rule

Humor must not erase depth.

After a vulnerable or meaningful prompt, the character line may lower pressure, but it must never mock:
- the answer;
- affection;
- apology;
- gratitude;
- a family member's discomfort.

The strongest pattern is:
1. real moment;
2. small release of pressure;
3. one memorable line.

Not every comment has to be louder than the page.
