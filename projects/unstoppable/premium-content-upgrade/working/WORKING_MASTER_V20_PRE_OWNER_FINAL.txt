WELCOME TO PROJECT UNSTOPPABLE
THIS IS NOT A SCHOOL BOOK.
It is a 31-day field guide for real life: hard starts,
awkward moments, friendship drama, big plans, bad days,
and the times when you need a better next move.
Think of it as your personal SURVIVAL KIT.
The Happy Makers brought missions, experiments, puzzles,
and tools you can actually use again.
No perfect streak required. No personality transplant.
YOU ARE THE CAPTAIN.
Test what helps. Keep what works.
READY? DAY 1 STARTS NOW.

1. THE PICK: Choose 3 values that matter to you right now.
Try: honesty, loyalty, humor, freedom, curiosity, kindness,
courage, creativity, fairness, independence. Add your own.

usually leaves you feeling worse about yourself.
No social media? Silence one comparison trigger: a
notification, channel, or feed you do not actually want.
Your feed gets screen time. It does not get voting rights.

DAY 2: STRENGTHS & GLITCHES
[Luli CONNECTING...]
A trait is not a permanent score. Stubborn can help when
you're finishing something hard and cause problems when
you stop listening. Quiet can mean unsure in one moment
and observant in another.

better move.
A label is a terrible user manual.
“
A TRAIT CAN FEEL LIKE A BUG IN ONE SITUATION

MAIN QUEST: The Context Switch
1. THE TRAITS: Pick 3 traits you sometimes wish you could
change.

2. HELPS WHEN: For each one, write when it can be useful.

3. GETS IN THE WAY WHEN: Write when the same trait can
make things harder.

when you ignore feedback / switch = pause and ask one
question.

1. Pick a low-stakes mean comment you have heard or read.
Do NOT use the worst thing anyone has ever said to you.

2. FACT:
Write the exact words — no extra story.

3. FILTER:
What does this comment NOT get to decide about you?

4. NEXT MOVE:
Choose what fits the situation:
IGNORE / MUTE / BLOCK / SAVE EVIDENCE / GET HELP.

■ XP BOOST ■

'Okay.' / 'Not doing this.' / no reply.
If it is repeated bullying, threatening, sexual,
discriminatory, or makes you feel unsafe, block/report it,
save evidence when you can, and tell a trusted adult or
school staff member.
