# Detective Academy — First 7-Day Owner Review Packet

Status: READY FOR OWNER REVIEW / DO NOT SCHEDULE YET
Created: 2026-09-23
Execution owner: RSE Marketing Autopilot
Launch trigger: verified live Amazon/KDP URL
Primary buyer: parent/caregiver of a child around 8–12
Primary goal: establish product difference + ownership before asking for purchase

## Batch rule

This first batch is deliberately built around three buyer questions:
1. Will my child actually want to engage with this?
2. Is this more than another puzzle/activity workbook?
3. What makes this book meaningfully different from the many similar Amazon options?

The batch does NOT lead with educational promises.
Adventure, role, continuity and discovery are the lead.
Critical thinking / screen-free / Hint Vault are supporting proof.

After owner approval:
- copy refinements, hook variants and routine native adaptations may run autonomously;
- scheduling may begin only after the verified KDP URL is available;
- paid activation remains owner-gated.
