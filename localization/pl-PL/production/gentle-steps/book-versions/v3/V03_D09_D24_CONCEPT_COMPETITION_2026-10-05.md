## D09-A — PLAN B: 60 SEKUND

### Core idea

A fast cooperative problem-solving game built from absurd-but-recognizable family micro-chaos.

No one "wins". The family wins if it can build one workable plan before time ends.

### Draft mechanic

**START:** Sit or stand together. The oldest child/adult reads the first situation.

**ROUND 1 — 60 seconds**
Situation:
> "You have five minutes to leave the house. One glove is missing, someone has just remembered they need to bring something to school tomorrow, the phone is on 3%, and the dog is sitting by the door with the leash."

Each person may add exactly **one action** to the plan on their turn.

Rules:
- you cannot undo someone else's idea;
- you may improve it or add the next step;
- every person must have one useful role before the round ends.

After everyone speaks, ask:
> "Would this actually get us out the door?"

If yes: point for the team.
If not: 20-second repair.

**ROUND 2**
A new situation:
> "Dinner is almost ready. The washing machine has just finished, someone cannot find the charger, tomorrow's school bag is not packed, and the doorbell rings."

Same rules.

**FINAL**
The family invents one own "classic at our place" situation and solves it together.

**TWO PEOPLE**
Exactly the same. Each person gets two turns per scenario instead of one.

### Why it fits Poland now

It converts the real overload of after-school schedules, household work and constant micro-logistics into play rather than another lecture about cooperation.

### Risk

Could feel too close to real life if examples are stressful rather than playful.
Must keep scenarios exaggerated and harmless.

### Jury score

- want-to-play: 9.5/10
- team value: 9.5/10
- 2-player: 10/10
- clarity: 9/10
- 5-minute fit: 9/10
- novelty: 10/10
- Polish fit: 10/10
- safety/inclusion: 10/10

**Weighted score: 96/100**

### Jury verdict

**WINNER**

Why:
It is the only candidate that creates real family teamwork without relying on body contact, synchronized movement, memory chains or another guessing mechanic. It also meets the owner's "family acts like a team" request through the game itself.

## D24-A — CZTERY MINUTY. CZTERY MISJE.

### Core idea

A four-part mini-gauntlet made only of callbacks to things the family already learned.

No new rules to learn on Day 24.

### Draft mechanic

**START**
Set a simple rule:
> Four missions. About one minute each. No points. The goal is to finish all four together.

### MISSION 1 — ZGRAJCIE SIĘ
Callback to team/synchrony days.

Without choosing a leader:
- everyone starts with hands on knees;
- together raise hands;
- together lower them;
- together stop.

Repeat three times.
The third time nobody counts aloud.

Works identically with two people.

### MISSION 2 — TYLKO PYTANIA: FINAŁ
Callback to D17.

For 30 seconds:
> "You are standing by the door and somebody has just said: 'Wait, where are the gifts?'"

Talk only in questions.

For 2 people: exactly the same.

### MISSION 3 — NA SZCZĘŚCIE / NIESTETY: EXPRESS
Callback to D23.

One lap only.
Opening:
> "We thought everything was finally ready for Christmas..."

Next person: "Na szczęście..."
Next: "Niestety..."
Continue until everyone has added one sentence.

With two: three exchanges.

### MISSION 4 — NIEWIDZIALNA PIŁKA: POWRÓT
Callback to Day 1.

Pass the invisible ball one last time.
This time every throw carries **one thing from these 24 days you want to keep**:
- a game;
- a line;
- a moment;
- a small habit;
- an inside joke.

One sentence maximum.

This bridges naturally into existing MIĘDZY NAMI without replacing it.

### ENCORE — optional

If the family wants:
sing/hum one chorus of a Christmas/winter song.
Nobody has to sing.

### Why it works

- opening callback to the very first game;
- middle callbacks to the strongest late-game mechanics;
- cooperative, verbal and emotional payoff;
- final invisible ball turns Day 1 into a bookend rather than a repeat;
- works with two;
- no materials;
- no one must perform solo;
- no new complex instructions on Christmas Eve.

### Local fit

It respects the reality that Day 24 is not the time for another complicated project. The final feels special because the family recognizes "their" mechanics, not because the book asks for preparation.

- 2-player: 10/10
- 10-minute fit: 9/10
- inclusivity: 10/10
- Polish fit: 9.5/10

**Weighted score: 96.5/100**

### Jury verdict
