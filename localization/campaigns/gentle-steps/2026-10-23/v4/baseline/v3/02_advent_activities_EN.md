# An Activity Advent Calendar That Doesn’t Give Parents More Work

The children are asleep. You remember tomorrow’s Advent envelope is supposed to contain an activity. What activity? And do you have everything you need for it?

There’s another way to do an activity Advent calendar. No cards to print, little gifts to buy or new ideas to come up with every night. **The surprise is what you’ll do together.**

**Before Christmas Slips By**, from Rise.Shine.Evolve. and The Happy Makers, gives families 24 ready-to-use days: a short pause, a game and something to talk about. Around ten minutes when the adults get to play, too.

## What’s inside?

Here are three small glimpses from **three different days**.

### LET’S TAKE A MOMENT

**One Sound Quieter.** The TV is on. Notifications keep chiming. Someone’s trying to tell a story. Don’t ask everyone to be quiet. Turn down one source of noise, such as the TV, for a minute. Listen to the room with a little less going on.

### LET’S DO THIS TOGETHER

**Reverse Charades.** One person guesses while everyone else acts out the same thing at once, without agreeing on the gestures. With “building a snowman,” three people may appear to be playing three different winter sports. That’s part of the fun.

### LET’S TALK

**“What little things have we picked up from each other?”**

A phrase your mum says. A song your child got stuck in everyone’s head. The way Grandad makes a sandwich. You start to notice how much of each other you carry around. Including that ridiculous expression nobody can stop saying.

## Open the book and you’re ready

The games include two-person versions. **TRY IT TOMORROW** suggests how to use that short pause again tomorrow. No extra shopping or activity prep.

Home late? Someone’s had enough? Skip a day. Nobody has to join, and there’s nothing to catch up on.

Maybe tomorrow’s question will be **“What are we doing together tonight?”**
