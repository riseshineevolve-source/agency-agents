## 3. BUYER PROBLEM

December is already full:
- school;
- work;
- after-school activities;
- traffic;
- shopping;
- gifts;
- food / hosting;
- packages;
- laundry;
- screens;
- things needed tomorrow.

Parents want Christmas to feel like Christmas without taking on another project.

The book should sound like relief, not another obligation.
