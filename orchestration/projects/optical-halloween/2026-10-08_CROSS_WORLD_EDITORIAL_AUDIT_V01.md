## High-priority issues and repairs by world
### House
1. **H-01 reflection proof:** a reflected key gaining teeth is magical unless its physical notches are shown as precisely aligned shapes from the scene environment. Make this an *anamorphic completion* with an explicit mirror angle, not extra literal metal magically drawn in reflection; the later real-threshold payoff must be visually comparable.
2. **H-02 / H-11 / H-18 shadow law:** early footsteps, the cat’s door-shaped shadow, and arriving chair shadow are three different shadow events. Define the single house rule: interrupted gestures show their *destination* before the actual movement. Each event should be causally separate and use correct cast direction except for the one bound difference.
3. **H-06 future reflection overlaps Museum:** the teacup must point toward an *unfinished prepared place*, not an unrestricted vision of an alternate future. The Museum exclusively owns future-reflecting glass as its primary rule.
4. **H-09 through H-12 camera risk:** chair → bent staircase → cat shadow → inside-facing window could become four ornate room views. Make the chair high overhead, stair a sharply sectional object, shadow very low and window an exterior-seeming cutaway; reject repeated front corridor compositions.
5. **H-13 gloves overlap M-05 gloves:** make House’s gloves humble physical objects left for an invitation; Museum’s gloves should be sculptural silhouettes of an unrealized dance. Different scale, material and camera.
6. **H-20 reveal proof:** revisiting the H-01 door must show tangible before/after differences in geometry and light direction. No generic happy porch sunrise; no written invitation text.

### Carnival
7. **C-01 ticket physics:** materialization of light is the one fantasy gesture. Keep a visible actual lamp, a specific polished plane and a clipped bracket silhouette; do not make ticket float until Mara takes it. The same missing beat-shaped corner must remain consistent through C-18.
8. **C-05 / C-06 / C-11 loop risk:** carousel rotation, organ keys and wheel spokes are three nearby circular/rhythmic devices. Use radically different camera grammar and material: carousel single large axle cutaway, organ stair-like close-up, wheel as floor-projected shadow with no big full wheel repeated.
9. **C-07 mirror jump:** an acrobat’s reflection cannot contain unrelated limbs, positions or a wholly separate performer. If the mirror shows one future landing pose, restrict that deviation to a single glass/floor field with a clear physical source.
10. **C-09 audience silhouettes:** shadows on empty seats should not accidentally imply a creepy murdered crowd. They stand for *held applause*, with anatomically plausible seated adult body profiles and one deliberate gap.
11. **C-10 ringmaster:** allow the distant ringmaster no villain portrait, monster silhouette or stereotypical circus-horror makeup; the held baton later becomes a protective safety gesture, not mind control.
12. **C-13 to C-18 causality:** the stage mechanism must not solve a character issue on its own. Mara’s reciprocal clap is a direct choice, the audience responds, and the performer finishes. Avoid adding a generic magical clock or collectible 'beat crystal'.
13. **C-20 dawn visual:** do not rely on a realistic sunrise, natural sky, or 'happy end' illustration. Show the established stage geometry unfolding *outward* and the ticket’s former missing corner resolved through a new human gesture.

### Museum
14. **M-02 animal-portrait duplication:** crow must be a small glass sculpture/silhouette, not a gorgeous large animal portrait repeating Optical Animals or the House’s adult black cat.
15. **M-04 future-only glass:** all prospective alternate outcomes must be bounded by a named vitrined surface; other objects in the world remain in physical present time. The illusion is less striking if every surface is inexplicably prophetic.
16. **M-05 vs H-13:** the borrowed gloves should describe the gesture of a dance, suspended in an exhibit with generous black-and-white sculptural forms, unlike House household gloves.
17. **M-08 umbrella overlaps H-04:** replace or redesign the umbrella as an array of *frozen refraction lenses and suspended unfelt raindrops*, with no umbrella hero; the House owns the upside-down umbrella silhouette.
18. **M-09–M-12 variation:** instrument, chandelier, portrait, vacant display must not all share symmetrical nave/cathedral camera. At least two should be asymmetrical object close-ups, underside views or overhead gallery cuts.
19. **M-11 portrait truth:** a negative-space sitter should emerge only through the physical viewing angle or reflected surface. Do not draw a literal paranormal portrait face hovering in empty air.
20. **M-12 / M-13 reveal timing:** the empty case may *appear* to hold the visitor’s outline, but M-13 must prove it is an optical placement rather than permanent imprisonment. Show the actual visitor outside the glass or provide another geometrically verifiable source.
21. **M-19 eclipse motif:** do not center another moon/bullseye. Make the once-missing piece a seam in existing architecture that now permits a path of white light; M-20 can release it through the lantern.
