## Audit rule

Review all 24 conversation tasks as real family moments, not as generic reflection prompts.

Check:
- naturalness in a Polish family;
- no forced vulnerability or praise;
- no workshop/coaching feel;
- no function repetition;
- child 8–12 anti-cringe;
- adult participation without embarrassment;
- comment logic and character voice;
- whether humor is earned by the exact situation.

KEEP beats rewrite.
