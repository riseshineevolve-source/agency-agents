## Option A

# ŚWIĘTA SĄ TEŻ PO DRODZE

**Rodzinny kalendarz adwentowy na 24 dni. Kilka minut dziennie, żeby porozmawiać, pośmiać się i po prostu pobyć razem.**

Cover claim:
**24 dni • do 10 minut dziennie**

Positioning note:
Warm, family-first, distinctive and brandable. Carries the mindful/present-together idea without mindfulness language or an English-calque structure.

## Option B

# ZANIM ZNOWU BĘDZIE PO ŚWIĘTACH

**Rodzinny kalendarz adwentowy na 24 dni. Codziennie kilka minut na rozmowę, zabawę i chwile, które naprawdę warto zapamiętać.**

Cover claim:
**24 dni • do 10 minut dziennie**

Positioning note:
Highly relatable to the Polish December experience: weeks of preparation and the feeling that the holidays suddenly pass. Stronger emotional/FOMO edge, but still warm and family-focused.

## Shared title rules

- Polish title must read as an originally Polish book title, not a translation of *24 Gentle Steps to Christmas*.
- Keep **kalendarz adwentowy** in the subtitle for discoverability/SEO.
- Do not use literal mindfulness terminology in the title.
- Do not use a title that falsely suggests December itself becomes calm or unhurried.
- The short-time promise belongs primarily in the cover claim: **24 dni • do 10 minut dziennie**.
- Final title remains an explicit owner gate.
