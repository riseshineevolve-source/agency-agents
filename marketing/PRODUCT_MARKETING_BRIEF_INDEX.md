# RSE Product Marketing Brief Index

Status: ACTIVE
Updated: 2026-10-07

## Rule

Every actively marketed RSE product must have one canonical Product Marketing Brief.

Purpose:
- prevent title / subtitle / positioning drift;
- prevent slogans and keyword research from being lost in chats;
- keep one durable buyer-language / keyword / hook source;
- separate current locks from historical experiments;
- let Marketing Autopilot resume without reconstructing context.

Template:
`orchestration/templates/RSE_PRODUCT_MARKETING_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md`

When a product has a brief, that brief overrides older product-specific marketing copy on:
- current title/subtitle;
- positioning;
- slogan status;
- keyword direction;
- current metadata;
- copy bans;
- market/language adaptation.

## Minimum completion standard

A brief is not COMPLETE until it records:
1. exact product name / title / subtitle;
2. current source-of-truth content/artifact;
3. target buyer;
4. buyer problem;
5. positioning;
6. strongest slogans/hooks;
7. keyword clusters;
8. market-language differences;
9. product proof / claims;
10. CTAs;
11. copy bans;
12. current owner locks / live-verification gates.

## Operating rule

Do not delete old copy packs or research.
The Product Marketing Brief is the current decision layer placed above historical material.

Historical files remain useful for:
- alternate hooks;
- rejected title challengers;
- previous experiments;
- performance comparison;
- asset reuse.
