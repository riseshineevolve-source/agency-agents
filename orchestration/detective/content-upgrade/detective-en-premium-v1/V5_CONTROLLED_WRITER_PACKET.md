## North-star

It must read like a premium middle-grade detective adventure whose puzzles are part of the plot, not worksheets inserted between story paragraphs.

The reader should feel:
- recruited, not lectured;
- useful, not praised;
- inside the team, not watching it;
- curious about Room Zero from the first pages;
- increasingly aware that earlier work matters;
- surprised by twists that were fairly seeded.

## Revision strategy

Work by story arcs, not random cases:
A. Opening + Cases 01–05
B. Cases 06–09
C. Cases 10–15
D. Cases 16–21

F. Cases 26–30 + Room Zero + Archive File 001

- compare changed segments to allowlist;
- verify protected mechanics;
- preserve strongest existing lines;
- checkpoint.

One writer. No parallel prose mutation.
