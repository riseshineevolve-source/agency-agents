## Why this direction is selected

- immediately identifies the product as a **rodzinny kalendarz adwentowy**;
- keeps the **24-day** format explicit;
- communicates the strongest low-friction product truth: **nothing to buy, nothing to prepare**;
- avoids implying crafts, shopping, materials or extra work for the parent;
- avoids making the book sound like it is only conversation;
- preserves the core promise of short shared family time;
- `Tylko WY. 10 minut razem.` is intentionally used as cover-style rhythmic copy rather than a long explanatory sentence.
