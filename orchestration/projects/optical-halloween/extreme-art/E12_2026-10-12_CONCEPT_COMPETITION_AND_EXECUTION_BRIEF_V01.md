Pros: clear scale shock, one optical device, real form-pattern continuity, large colorable glove whites, ambitious low view. Cons: anatomy and five arch alignment require manual review. No door icons printed on fingertips, no lettering or numbered clues. 40–55% solid black target, pure binary after deterministic conversion. Woven ribbons follow actual finger joints, do not simulate shadowy gradient. No stock hall, checkerboard-floor default, spiral/staircase default, mandala, chibi, second character, or detached sticker hand. The missing-minute clue is a TINY EMPTY picture frame at far vanishing point, not text.
