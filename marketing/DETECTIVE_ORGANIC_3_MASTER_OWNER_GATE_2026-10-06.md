## MASTER 03 — STUCK IS PART OF DETECTIVE WORK

### Buyer state
Parent who wants challenge but worries the book may become frustrating or require constant adult help.

### Blocker hypothesis
The buyer assumes a hard puzzle book gives either no support or gives away the answer too quickly.

### Hook
**STUCK IS PART OF DETECTIVE WORK.**

Supporting:
**A NUDGE WHEN THEY NEED IT. NOT THE ANSWER BEFORE THEY'RE READY.**

### Verified proof
The product includes a 3-level Hint Vault with progressively stronger guidance before the full solution.

### Source assets
Preferred:
- `A_PLUS_03_HINT_VAULT_MASTER_1940x1200.png`
- exact current Hint Vault proof pages if/when the final interior crop is approved

Do not manufacture a fake hint screen.

### Core copy
Getting stuck is not a failure in detective work.

The useful skill is knowing what to try next.

Detective Academy uses a three-level Hint Vault:
- Level 1: what to notice;
- Level 2: where to focus;
- Level 3: a stronger nudge.

The full solution is there when the reader wants it — not before.

### CTA
Prelaunch: **SAVE THIS FOR YOUR FUTURE DETECTIVE.**
Live: **OPEN CASE 01.**

### Platform adaptation
Instagram / Facebook:
- 4-slide carousel;
- each hint level gets one slide;
- final slide = “The answer is there. It just does not arrive first.”

YouTube Short:
- kinetic-text version only if real Hint Vault crop remains current;
- otherwise skip video and keep static.

### Next hypothesis
Parent reassurance around independent challenge should improve saves and purchase-intent comments without making the product feel easy.
