The subject hierarchy written in the cards is essential, not optional decoration.

**Textual changes made:** M-11 was rewritten from a polished-floor discovery to a real parallax opening, breaking a three-reflection sequence while preserving the reason to inspect the case from below. **Continuity HOLD:** C-01 and C-02 below remain until sequential images preserve the same person, cabinet, bracket, and freedom of choice. The text supplies a defensible story, but does not prove that an image viewer will infer every causal beat without captions inside the art.
