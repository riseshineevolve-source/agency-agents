# Target Language and Style Profile — Project Unstoppable

Route: SAME_LANGUAGE_PREMIUM_UPGRADE
Language: English
Audience: 10–14

## Desired register
- clear premium book English, not chat transcription;
- friendly, warm, quick, specific;
- teen-world aware without slang cosplay;
- playful, dry, situational humor;
- short punch lines where they earn their place;
- practical actions over inspirational slogans;
- direct but never bossy or shaming.

## Happy Makers voice
**Dilo:** quick, direct, tech/game metaphors used selectively; dry punchline; respects the reader's intelligence.
**Luli:** socially observant, warm, sharp, curious; humor comes from noticing real-life awkwardness, not trend slang.
**Team:** celebratory and grounded; no hype inflation.

## Avoid
- therapy/workshop/corporate coaching voice;
- fake youth slang, dated meme language, TikTok cadence;
- baby language;
- forced vulnerability, gratitude, apology, forgiveness or disclosure;
- pseudo-neuroscience and unsupported mechanism claims;
- universal statements about why parents, bullies or readers behave a certain way;
- moralizing rich/poor language;
- repeated hack/algorithm/server metaphors when a normal sentence is stronger.

## Protected brand vocabulary (provisional)
MAIN QUEST; XP BOOST; TOOL FOR LIFE / RESCUE KIT; SYSTEM REBOOT; GLITCH & PUZZLE; FREE ROAM; Happy Makers; Dilo; Luli.

MINDFULNESS is not protected. It is under review because SYSTEM REBOOT already carries the brand function and the extra label often pulls the book toward a coaching/wellness register.
