## RSE marketing voice

Warm, clever, practical, playful where appropriate, never preachy, never generic AI copy.

For parents:
- zero guilt
- no fear amplification
- no unsupported neuroscience or therapeutic claims
- make use easy to imagine
- one clear next action

For children:
- agency, curiosity, challenge, discovery
- never "you need fixing"

## Content production model

Default:
- 3 strong master ideas per week
- adapt each master natively to IG / FB / YT / TikTok when connected
- repurpose winners before inventing unrelated content
- English master first; Polish after source lock

## Creative production ladder

Marketing must NOT become blocked by fragile AI video generation.

Use this priority order:
1. approved real product pages / mockups / interior previews
2. static carousel or poster with strong copy
3. simple motion/kinetic typography/page zoom/pan
4. light montage from approved assets
5. AI-generated video only when it clearly adds value and can be produced reliably

If AI video requires repeated manual retries or object-continuity repair, downgrade immediately to a lower-complexity format. Speed-to-market and product truth beat flashy generative video.
