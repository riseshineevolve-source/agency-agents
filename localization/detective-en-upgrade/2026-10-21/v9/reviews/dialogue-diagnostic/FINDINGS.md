# HM Comms: dialogue and humour diagnostic

Reviewer: `/root/hm9_dialogue_humor`. Diagnostic only, not a final approval. Root is the sole candidate writer.

All34dialogue units/183turns assessed. Original,V7,V8 compared for the30maincases; auxiliary scenes use the exactV8 text and are not falsely attributed to an unavailable original. Adjacent story checked for comic/character fit; protected puzzles were not independently re-solved in this review.

## Main finding

The problem is compressed conversational causality, not simply too few words. Some brilliant original and V7 tiny lines should remain tiny. Give them fuller premises, actual replies and a human reason to be said. Allow affectionate sharpness and a witty answer from the person teased.

## Comic arc and repetition

- Use a few longer conversational turns per scene instead of making every speech a one-line cap. Setup -> actual response -> change/escalation -> return to case.
- Do not stitch every original joke and every V7 joke together. Select a coherent shared premise and its best ending.
- Let Luli play along and admit a mistake, Mimi be clever and fallible, Dilo choose useful work, Alio defend a funny theory and make discoveries, Nini be perceptive without baby talk, Bibi be experienced without being infallible.
- Preserve affectionate bite: impressive start, cruelly reasonable, ruthless/cardboard can work when the teased person has an equal reply and remains competent. Do not flatten every barb into praise.
- Vary comic engines: competitive one-upmanship, overconfident plans, family memory, physical table clutter, affectionate pride, absurd negotiation, child/adult reversal. Use formal evidence language as spice.
- Object personification/office bureaucracy currently occurs across cups, boxes, locker, spoon, walls, robot cable, phone, parrot, fox, watch, ghost, pear, instrument cases, scoreboard, parcel, empty-room witness and helmet. Do not add more everywhere; make jokes reveal relationships and decisions.
- Recurring props should develop: tunnel/minivan -> evidence card/decorative confidence; helmet -> changed department, not unrelated hat gags; missing phone -> oat-bar payoff; Dilo confidence -> corrected watch -> careful help after paper tear -> earned quiet at final map.
- Case21 should remain a gentle recovery scene, and Case29 a patient shared pause. Emotional contrast makes the louder jokes stronger.
- Welcome the reader through specific shared work in selected scenes, not a Detective slogan appended to every scene. Never add a new task or puzzle fact.
- Restore actual original kernels only where they are strongest. Reject older mechanical defects and weak lines: photo-hat mismatch, unsupported who-carried-box question, vague careers-later cap, unclear empty-bedroom precedent.
