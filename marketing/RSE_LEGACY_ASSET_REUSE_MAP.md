## Reuse policy
Do not publish an old asset because it exists.
Every asset must pass:
1. current product truth
2. current character/cover/logo lock
3. mobile readability
4. one clear idea
5. one CTA
6. current claims policy
7. visual freshness

## Repackage rule
A weak old execution with a strong idea is not trash.
Extract:
- visual mechanic
- hook
- camera move
- character beat
- CTA pattern
- interaction concept

Then rebuild with current assets.
