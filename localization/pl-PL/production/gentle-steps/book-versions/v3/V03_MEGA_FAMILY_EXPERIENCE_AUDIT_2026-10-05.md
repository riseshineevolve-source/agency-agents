# Priority findings

## P1 — fix before content freeze

### P1-01 — D01 ZWOLNIJ: "pilne" logic defect

Current wording:
> "Jeśli w trakcie przypomni wam się coś pilnego, zostawcie to na później."

Problem:
If something is genuinely urgent, "leave it until later" is logically wrong and undermines the otherwise careful practical tone.

Recommended direction:
Use "coś do zrobienia" / "jakaś rzecz z listy" rather than "coś pilnego".

This is a true logic fix, not style polish.

---

### P1-02 — D09 GRAMY: Human Knot is the weakest structural game

Problems:
1. Core value works mainly for ~4–6 people.
2. Three-person version has little actual knot/challenge.
3. Two-person version is mechanically thin.
4. Touch/no-touch consent is now correctly handled, but the no-touch fallback duplicates D20 almost directly:
   - alphabetical order;
   - birth-month order;
   - silent arranging.
5. The instruction/safety burden is high relative to payoff.
6. For a book promising easy family participation, this is one of the likeliest "skip this tonight" pages.

Verdict:
**REDESIGN, not more wording polish.**

Desired replacement:
A genuinely cooperative 5-minute team challenge that:
- works equally well for 2, 3, 4, 5+;
- needs no props or touch;
- creates interdependence and shared success;
- does not duplicate D5/D6/D10/D15/D18/D20.

---

### P1-03 — D24 GRAMY: finale does not feel like a finale

Current:
**ŚPIEWAMY RAZEM**

Strength:
Warm, inclusive, ritual-like.

Problem:
As Day 24 it is less ambitious and less memorable than several earlier games. A family can legally "participate" by simply listening. That is good accessibility, but weak culmination.

Arc result:
The book emotionally closes, but the **game layer does not peak**.

Recommendation:
Do not necessarily delete singing. Consider:
- stronger final shared mission first;
- singing/ritual as closing beat;
or
- a final challenge that uses callbacks to several earlier game/tool mechanics.

Day 24 should feel:
"we made it through 24 days and this is ours",
not merely:
"now sing a song together".

Verdict:
**REDESIGN / MAJOR UPGRADE.**

---

### P1-04 — D05 two-person mechanics are weaker than advertised

Group version of leaderless counting works because multiple people can collide.

With two players:
- after one player speaks, the natural next speaker is almost obvious;
- the "who speaks next?" tension collapses;
- Level 2/3 are therefore much less meaningful.

Current line:
"Gracie tak samo. ... nie ustalajcie z góry, kto powie następną liczbę."

That is technically clear but not a convincing two-player game.

Recommendation:
Design a real 2-person variant, e.g. introduce:
- unpredictable allowed repeats;
- gesture/sound alternation;
- forbidden numbers;
- variable turn ownership triggered by signal;
without turning it into a different, overcomplicated game.

---

### P1-05 — Back matter continuity contradiction

Back matter says:
> "Kiedy dom zacznie brzmieć za głośno, możecie przypomnieć sobie jedną minutę ciszy."

But D21 explicitly teaches:
**not ideal silence; remove one sound layer**.

This is a direct philosophy/continuity mismatch.

Recommended direction:
Reference "jeden dźwięk mniej" instead of "minuta ciszy".

# P2 — materially improve if we do one more premium pass

### P2-01 — D18 time/space scaling

MISJA KRZESŁO is strong:
- clear communication;
- trust;
- explicit no-speed rule;
- open eyes are fully valid.

But with a larger family and only one safe route:
- both roles for every person can push the day beyond 10 minutes;
- simultaneous pairs require enough room, which many homes may not have.

Recommendation:
Add a clear time-saving rule:
- one round per pair tonight;
- second role only if time/space allows;
or explicitly allow pairs to use separate micro-routes.

---

### P2-02 — D01 can exceed 10 minutes

NIEWIDZIALNA PIŁKA is an excellent opening game, but:
- physical round;
- full compliment round;
- MIĘDZY NAMI with two answers per person;
make Day 1 one of the longest experiences.

This is acceptable if intentional, but Day 1 should not accidentally break the "about 10 minutes" promise.

Recommendation:
Do not cut the hero content reflexively.
Instead make one element clearly optional / "if you have another minute".

---

### P2-03 — D23 can exceed 10 minutes when apology is real

The game is good and MIĘDZY NAMI is intentionally optional.

But a sincere apology can naturally become a conversation longer than 2 minutes.

This is not a reason to remove it.

Recommendation:
The product promise should tolerate that this is one of the few days where a real conversation may outgrow the timer.
Could add a subtle cue that the 10-minute structure can end, while the conversation may continue privately if needed.

---

### P2-04 — D13 "Ekspert na poczekaniu" has spotlight pressure

It is a strong word/improv game and much better than the old Red Carpet.

But 20 seconds of solo "expert performance" can still be unpleasant for a shy 8–12-year-old or adult.

Recommendation:
Add a no-drama alternative:
- pair of co-experts;
- one PASS allowed;
or "you may answer in one sentence".

Do not dilute the game; just preserve agency.

---

### P2-05 — D19 is a late-book energy dip

The issue is placement:

Day 20 is non-verbal team coordination.
D19 between them feels like an earlier-book observation game.

This can work as a breather, but if the desired arc is "Advent grows stronger/deeper", D19 slightly lowers perceived ambition.

Owner decision:

or
- upgrade to a stronger observation/team version.

Gesture telephone is fun with a chain.
With two people it becomes a simple "show once / copy once" memory task.

It is playable, but the magic is reduced.

Not a redesign priority unless we are already touching D7.

# Progression / Advent arc

## ZWOLNIJ progression — PASS

Early:
basic pause / breath / sensory narrowing.

Middle:
body tension, pace, touch/space, slowing ordinary movement.

Late:
portable self-use tools, less sensory overload, dropping nonurgent tasks, closing the day.

The late toolbox is generally stronger than the early toolbox.

## MIĘDZY NAMI progression — STRONG PASS

There is a visible move:
daily observation -> family knowledge -> strengths/team -> memory/home -> repair -> wish.

D23 apology is a genuine depth peak.

## GRAMY progression — PARTIAL PASS

The book becomes **more varied**, but not consistently more ambitious.

Peaks:
- D10;
- D15–18;
- D20;
- D22–23.

Dip:
- D19.

Problem:
D24 drops from late-book challenge into a simple song.

This means the whole Advent does not finish on its strongest gameplay beat.

## Recommended arc philosophy

Do not force every day to be harder than the previous day.

Use a wave:
- build;
- breathe;
- build;
- peak;
- close.

But Week 4 should clearly feel like "final week".

Suggested final-week shape:
- D19: lighter breather is acceptable;
- D20: team;
- D21: verbal;
- D22: laughter peak;
- D23: improv + repair/depth;
- D24: **culmination / callback / celebration**.

Current D24 does not yet deliver that culmination.

# Finale audit

## What is already strong

- OSTATNIA MINUTA is emotionally right.
- D24 MIĘDZY NAMI works as a close:
  - one thing worth repeating;
  - one wish.
- Happy Makers closing line is strong:
  **"Strona 24 się kończy. Wasze najlepsze pomysły nie muszą."**
- Back matter supports continuation without turning it into a January program.

## What is missing

A signature final shared experience.

The final should ideally:
- reference skills/fun from earlier days;
- involve everyone;
- create one memory/photo-worthy moment without requiring a photo;
- work for 2 people;
- be low-prep;
- preserve non-singer/non-performer inclusion;
- feel unmistakably like "Day 24".

Possible design direction, not yet a proposed patch:
**HAPPY MAKERS FINAL MISSION**
A 5-minute cooperative mini-finale combining 3 callbacks:
- one nonverbal coordination beat;
- one quick verbal/improv beat;
- one family callback/inside joke;
then optional song/refren as celebration.

This would create payoff rather than a brand-new unrelated game.
