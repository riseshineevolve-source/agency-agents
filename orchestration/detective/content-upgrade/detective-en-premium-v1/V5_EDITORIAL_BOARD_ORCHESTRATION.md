Status: ACTIVE

## Operating law

This stream follows:

**MANY INDEPENDENT REVIEWERS -> ONE EDITOR-IN-CHIEF -> ONE CONTROLLED WRITER -> INDEPENDENT VALIDATION**

No reviewer writes into the master.
No parallel writers.
No whole-book rewrite by multiple agents.
No puzzle/mechanics changes.

## Why this board exists

V4 fixed source convergence with the current illustrated opening and preserved the full puzzle/twist chain, but the manuscript still needs a stronger continuous detective-story experience:
- more causal action between cases;
- clearer reason WHY each deduction matters;
- stronger reader belonging without repetitive "welcome detective" language;
- better middle-book momentum;
- less "case queue" feeling;
- stronger suspense / payoff rhythm;
- sharper character-specific humor;
- no loss of existing strong copy or fair-play twists.

## Reviewers

### 1. Narrative Designer — STORY SYSTEMS
Source agent: `game-development/narrative-designer.md`

Reviews:
- five-act momentum;
- case-to-case causality;
- story/gameplay integration;
- delayed payoffs;
- setup/payoff visibility;
- scene vs. exposition balance;
- whether solving a case produces a felt consequence.

Does NOT rewrite the master.

### 2. Book Co-Author — LONG-FORM RED THREAD
Source agent: `marketing/marketing-book-co-author.md`

Adapted remit:
- book-level red thread;
- section function;
- scene/tension specificity;
- filler removal;
- "stack of files" vs. one coherent story;
- opening / middle / ending promise.

Does NOT import business-book tone.

### 3. Psychologist — AGE / MOTIVATION / BELONGING
Source agent: `academic/academic-psychologist.md`

Reviews:
- ages 8–12 cognitive fit;
- competence and agency;
- belonging and team attachment;
- suspense without threat;
- frustration / reward cadence;
- whether the reader feels like a teammate rather than a student.

Does NOT pathologize characters or add therapy language.

### 4. Reality Checker — LOGIC / CONTINUITY / FAIR PLAY
Source agent: `testing/testing-reality-checker.md`

Reviews:
- why each case happens now;
- why the requested deduction matters;
- whether narrative claims match puzzle truth;
- chronology;
- terminology;
- callbacks;
- fair-play twist seeds;
- contradiction hunting.

Does NOT change mechanics.

### 5. Character + Humor Reviewer — PROJECT-SPECIFIC
Local role, reviewer only.

Reviews:
- six voice identities;
- banter economy;
- repeated joke mechanics;
- anti-cringe;
- whether humor supports tension and relationship;
- whether a silence would land better than another joke.

Does NOT rewrite the master.

### 6. Puzzle Guardian — DETERMINISTIC
Not a creative writer.

Protects:
- titles where locked;
- objectives;
- rules;
- witness facts;
- evidence surfaces;
- coordinates;
- answers;
- Hint Vault truth;
- Solution Files truth;
- Case 03 exact ten;
- Case 05 sequence;
- Case 21 message;
- Case 26 extraction;
- Rule Zero;
- Case 29 D3;
- Case 30 final fields.

## Editor-in-Chief

The RSE Technical Orchestrator:
- compares reviewer findings;
- resolves conflicts using owner lock priority;
- turns reviews into one issue ledger;
- declares exact allowlist;
- protects GOLDEN_KEEP;
- writes one Writer Packet;
- approves only material improvements.

## One controlled writer

Role:
**Middle-Grade Mystery Writer**

Only this role may edit the working master after synthesis.

The writer receives:
- V4 baseline;
- immutable facts;
- twist map;
- character voice bible;
- exact issue ledger;
- allowed_changed_segments;
- protected hashes;
- Writer Packet.

The writer may not:
- alter puzzle truth;
- redesign a puzzle;
- change an answer;
- add a twist not seeded and approved;
- "improve" a protected line without a documented defect.
