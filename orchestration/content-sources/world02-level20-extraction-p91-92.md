### // FILE: 059 — THE TOOL ('The Glitch Spotter')

**The Science:** How to spot a fake?

- Hands: AI is famously bad at drawing fingers (too many, too few, weird joints).
- Shadows: Do they match the light source in the background?
- Source: Who posted it? A real scientist or an account named 'CoolGamer99'?

**In The Story:** Alio used his training to Pause and Zoom, noticing the five human fingers on a polar bear's paw.
