# RSE PRODUCT MARKETING BRIEF TEMPLATE

Status: TEMPLATE
Owner: Rise.Shine.Evolve

Purpose:
Every commercial RSE product should have one durable product marketing brief that survives chat/session changes.

The brief is the marketing source of truth for:
- product identity;
- title/subtitle;
- positioning;
- slogans/hooks;
- audience;
- keywords;
- metadata;
- proof points;
- CTAs;
- copy bans;
- channel use;
- current owner locks.

Do not reconstruct these from chat memory if a product brief exists.
