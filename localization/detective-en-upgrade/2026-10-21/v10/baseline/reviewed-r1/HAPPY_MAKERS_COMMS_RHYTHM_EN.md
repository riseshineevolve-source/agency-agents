# Happy Makers Comms

The Mystery of Room Zero


## Meet the Happy Makers

**MIMI:** All right. Files ready, team ready... where's my phone?

**LULI:** You said, 'Nobody move anything.' We took you seriously.

**DILO:** Do we get detective badges for finding it, or is that still a household chore?


## Your Case Wall

**DILO:** I was thinking my best theories could go across the top. In big letters.

**LULI:** How big are the facts allowed to be?

**ALIO:** Mine can go underneath Dilo's. Then mine will look more reasonable.

**NINI:** Could I have that little corner? I want to write 'We don't know yet.'

**BIBI:** Leave her plenty of room. That's usually our biggest category.

**MIMI:** Everyone step back a little. I'd like our new detective to see the wall before we fill it with ourselves.


## Case 01

**MIMI:** The envelope came from inside the Academy. No courier. That gives us somewhere to start.

**ALIO:** I've already got a secret tunnel and a minivan. We could start there.

**LULI:** You've arranged transport before finding a single clue?

**NINI:** He drew the minivan first. It's got all our names on the seats.

**DILO:** Then I want a window. I can watch for the evidence on the way.


## Case 02

**ALIO:** Look how neatly the Cup's sitting there. That is exactly how I put things back when I hope nobody asks where they've been.

**LULI:** We know. You line your shoes up after you've used someone's good scissors.

**ALIO:** That happened once. And this Cup is much tidier than my shoes.

**MIMI:** Well, I'm glad we've cleared the scissors up. The Cup still has a blank return sheet.

**NINI:** And no pockets. We should probably ask someone who was in the room.


## Case 03

**NINI:** I've found a sparkle on the trophy shelf. I'm calling it Inspector Glitter.

**LULI:** Has Inspector Glitter found any of our ten differences?

**NINI:** Not yet. It likes that shelf too much to look anywhere else.

**DILO:** I could do that job. Stand somewhere shiny and look important.

**LULI:** You'd want a chair by lunchtime. Pass me the other photo.


## Case 04

**DILO:** I'll watch the evidence box. Alio, you can patrol the tents.

**MIMI:** That's generous. You get to sit down while he does all the walking.

**DILO:** Someone has to stay with the box. I'm prepared to make that sacrifice.

**NINI:** I'll find out who was with Pixel. They might actually have something to tell us.

**ALIO:** Can I make my report sitting down, or is that a senior detective skill?

**MIMI:** Sit beside your brother. I'll patrol. Apparently it takes two of you to stop a sealed box going anywhere.


## Case 05

**DILO:** Six symbols. One locker. Detective, shall we see which of us gets it first?

**LULI:** If the Detective solves it, are you going to say you were just about to?

**DILO:** Only if I was. Which I might be. It's important to keep that possibility open.

**LULI:** I'll leave a little space beside the answer for 'Dilo was nearly there.'

**NINI:** What if the locker beats both of you? Does it get a badge?

**DILO:** No. It already knows the answer. That's a very unfair advantage.


## Four Matching Marks

**DILO:** Four matching marks, and still no explanation. Could the printer send a nice ordinary answer for once?

**LULI:** You'd turn it over to look for the puzzle on the back.

**NINI:** I'd settle for a name. My notes say 'the thing from the thing.' I knew what I meant when I wrote it.

**BIBI:** Bring them to the archive with me. We can look for proper names while Dilo checks the backs of things.


## Case 06

**NINI:** I'd already drawn the dragon. Now I've got a perfectly good dragon and a case about a spoon.

**LULI:** You could give it some soup.

**NINI:** That works. It can be a dragon who cooks.

**DILO:** I'm interested again. Does it breathe fire into the pan, or is that why it needs the spoon?

**LULI:** I was trying to help Nini finish a drawing. I seem to have started a cookbook.

**BIBI:** Leave me a page. I've burned enough dinners to contribute.

**NINI:** Grandma! You're supposed to be helping with the real history.

**BIBI:** I am. Those dinners happened.


## Case 07

**MIMI:** Alio, was that the third cupboard or the fourth? I'm starting to recognize the brooms.

**ALIO:** Fourth. I checked behind the coats as well.

**NINI:** That moon costume wouldn't fit behind the coats. It barely fits through a door.

**ALIO:** Then we've ruled out four cupboards. And all those coats.

**MIMI:** Splendid. Let's leave before I start calling the brooms by name.


## Case 08

**ALIO:** My shortest route gets us there in almost no time. I even drew it in one stroke.

**MIMI:** It also gets us to a locked door. What happens to all that time you saved?

**ALIO:** Small delay while we reconsider.

**MIMI:** Would you like to reconsider here, or after we've all followed you there?

**DILO:** Here, please. I know his reconsidering face. I'd rather see it sitting down.


## Case 09

**DILO:** You stopped halfway through saying something. Do you know that mark?

**BIBI:** I remember something about it. I want to check before I let you all run off with my memory.

**LULI:** I'll get the records. You can guard Alio while I'm gone.

**ALIO:** While we're checking memories, I distinctly remember being promised cake on this investigation.

**BIBI:** You promised yourself cake. Quite loudly. Nobody had a chance to agree.

**ALIO:** Then I need a better witness. Mine keeps agreeing with Grandma.


## Case 10

**DILO:** Two teams claiming one rover. I could keep it here while we sort this out.

**LULI:** Here beside you? How convenient.

**DILO:** I know how to look after a rover. I could even fix that cable.

**LULI:** Dilo, you haven't looked at either ownership sheet. You've already found room for it next to your chair.

**DILO:** Fine. Sheets first. But if anyone needs a temporary rover sitter, I'm still available.


## Case 11

**NINI:** Your phone has been living beside the oat bar.

**MIMI:** It was undercover.

**NINI:** Does the oat bar know?

**MIMI:** We protect our sources.

**DILO:** Can I put that in the bag report? Right beside 'Always know where your equipment is.'

**MIMI:** You would not. Pass me my source. I haven't had breakfast.


## Case 12

**DILO:** For the record: where did you hear those four words?

**NINI:** It's asking for a cracker, Dilo.

**DILO:** I heard. I've written it down three times. I was hoping we'd get to the password.

**NINI:** It thinks you're taking its order.

**DILO:** Right. I'll ask the handler. First witness who's made me feel like a slow waiter.


## Case 13

**ALIO:** My fox theory has one weakness. I'm trying to work around it.

**LULI:** Pockets?

**ALIO:** Exactly. Where does a fox keep a camera?

**NINI:** It needs a little strap. Here, I'll draw one around its neck.

**ALIO:** Thank you. Someone has finally taken the equipment problem seriously.

**LULI:** Lovely. Now I want to see the fox. This is why I shouldn't sit next to you two.


## Case 14

**DILO:** Somebody sounds very sure about a journey that doesn't seem to fit.

**NINI:** Maybe they've mixed up two moments. I've done that.

**LULI:** So have I. I was absolutely certain I'd packed lunch once. Three books. Not a sandwich in sight.

**MIMI:** I remember. You kept checking the bag, as if lunch might be in a different chapter.

**ALIO:** Three books? Surely one of them had something to eat in it.

**LULI:** A magnificent feast. I read it while Mom gave me half her sandwich.


## Case 15

**MIMI:** Next trip, big readable name tags. On every backpack.

**ALIO:** I was going to suggest satellite tracking. We could find a bag anywhere on Earth.

**MIMI:** With a tag, I can find mine without asking you to charge anything.

**ALIO:** Fine. One for each bag. And a little one for your phone, since we're solving family problems.


## Case 16

**ALIO:** If it rains on our next case, I'm standing under that hat.

**BIBI:** Ask first. There may be a grandmother under it.

**MIMI:** She used to make me carry it in a separate bag. My sandwiches traveled underneath.

**BIBI:** And arrived in perfect condition. You're welcome.

**DILO:** So Alio's not wrong. We could all fit under there.

**BIBI:** You may visit. No one is moving in.


## Case 17

**ALIO:** Helmet on. Ready for the railway investigation.

**LULI:** We're in the ticket hall. The biggest danger so far is you turning around with that backpack.

**ALIO:** I've been giving you plenty of warning.

**BIBI:** Yes. 'Coming through' is why I'm standing over here with the tickets.

**DILO:** Did he pack anything besides the helmet?

**BIBI:** Oh, yes. A very small sandwich. He knows what takes priority.


## Case 18

**MIMI:** How's your watch taking the news that it's wrong?

**DILO:** Quietly. With dignity. I'm proud of it.

**NINI:** And its owner?

**DILO:** I'm getting there. I've crossed my old times out, haven't I?

**LULI:** You have. I'll check the new ones with you. We can use my watch while yours enjoys being dignified.


## Case 19

**DILO:** I was going to say 'digital ghost.'

**LULI:** Was?

**DILO:** I'm checking the scanner first. The ghost can wait outside.

**LULI:** You see, now you've made it harder for me to tease you. I had a whole speech ready.

**NINI:** You could lend the ghost a book while it waits.

**DILO:** A paper one. I'm not giving it the Wi-Fi password.


## Case 20

**NINI:** That paint smear looks like a very worried pear. See the eyebrows?

**MIMI:** I'd rather see who moved the panel. Before you give every splash a name.

**NINI:** Just the pear. It's called Pear.

**DILO:** Easy to remember. Does Pear have an alibi?

**MIMI:** The pear is paint, Dilo.

**NINI:** Strong alibi.


## Case 21

**NINI:** Leave them just there. We can still see the torn edges.

**DILO:** I only meant to catch it.

**LULI:** I know. Come around here. I need someone to check the joins with me.

**BIBI:** We'll put it in a sleeve when it fits. Keep the writing where we can see it.

**NINI:** Do you recognize it?

**BIBI:** It feels familiar. I'd like to find another sample before I say more.

**DILO:** I'll check this edge. You take the next one, Detective.


## Case 22

**ALIO:** I can tell the three cases apart now. That one was on the left.

**LULI:** Before you walked around the table, or after?

**ALIO:** Ah. I had a system until you asked that.

**NINI:** I like the labels. They stay on the same case when I walk around it.

**DILO:** Let's use those. I don't want to solve this again every time Alio changes seats.


## Case 23

**DILO:** The arcade scoreboard has challenged me personally.

**MIMI:** It displayed a number.

**DILO:** In a tone.

**LULI:** I've got the wristband file. Come on. I'll let you tell me how easily you could beat it on the way.

**DILO:** I could. Easily. You're not allowed to count that as me playing, though.


## Case 24

**ALIO:** I've paused my dramatic theory.

**LULI:** Voluntarily?

**ALIO:** Yes. I want to get a proper look at the prints before I say anything else.

**LULI:** Then I'll be quiet too.

**MIMI:** Both of you? I should get a photograph. Nobody at home will believe me.

**ALIO:** Footprints first. Put my good behavior next to you finding your phone. I'd like the family album to be fair.


## Case 25

**NINI:** Everyone who touched this parcel was helping. It must be exhausting to be that well looked after.

**MIMI:** Let's give it a rest. Nobody move it until we know where it belongs.

**DILO:** I'll stay with it. Alio, if you're about to help, put your hands in your pockets.

**ALIO:** I was going to get us some cookies. Difficult now.

**MIMI:** Hands out, then. That's the first delivery here with a clear destination.


## The Old Cases Come Back

**NINI:** Fourteen selected maps. I knew we'd need all that careful filing.

**DILO:** I kept mine because the stack looked impressive. I've been showing people.

**LULI:** That explains why you asked if the librarian was coming round.

**BIBI:** Let him show it off. He's usually showing me something he hasn't finished yet.

**MIMI:** He can show them my elbow. It's been holding this corner down for ten minutes.


## Case 26

**NINI:** We've been looking at where everyone stood. Now it's the rooms with nobody in them.

**DILO:** Nobody's been very patient. Hasn't interrupted once.

**LULI:** Already my favorite witness.

**MIMI:** Luli, you once explained a footnote all the way through dinner. Nobody didn't get a word in then, either.

**BIBI:** I enjoyed that footnote. But I would like to hear what we've missed.


## Case 27

**BIBI:** Clean hands, please. And no folding the old map to make room for your elbows.

**ALIO:** May I point? I've got a very clean finger and nowhere else to put my excitement.

**BIBI:** Above the sleeve. Go on. I want to see what you've noticed.

**DILO:** All those cases, and he's finally been trusted with one finger.

**BIBI:** I trust the rest of him too. I just prefer it farther from my map.


## Case 28

**ALIO:** I've put my tunnel theory on a separate card. With a proper heading. It looks quite convincing.

**LULI:** How much evidence is on the card?

**ALIO:** At present? Decorative confidence.

**MIMI:** I'd like a card. I've put that into several very organized plans.

**BIBI:** Make it two. I used to call mine 'experience.'


## Case 29

**MIMI:** That's a lot of map. Slide the folders over. Let's give our Detective some room.

**NINI:** Take your time. I'll stay at this end so it doesn't curl up on you.

**DILO:** I can be quiet for one map. If that helps.

**LULI:** It does. I'll save anything worth teasing you about until afterwards.

**BIBI:** There. All of us here, and space to think. Begin when you're ready.


## Case 30

**MIMI:** Case Wall ready? Credential too? Then the panel's yours, Detective.

**DILO:** And the victory noise is mine. I've been saving a good one.

**LULI:** Keep it in your head until the door opens.

**NINI:** Mine's in there too. It's getting very crowded.

**BIBI:** Go on. You've brought us this far. We're right behind you.
