## Store / landing-page copy variants

### Short
24 days. About ten minutes a day. One simple rhythm: pause, play, connect.

Choose the format that fits your family's December — book, ebook or app — once each live format is verified.

### Warm
December rarely needs another project. It may need one small moment that already knows what to do.

Each of the 24 days gives your family a Mindful Moment, a Fun Spark and a Connection Share — about ten minutes, with no prep, extra shopping or cleanup.

### Practical
No elaborate setup. No craft-supply list. No pressure to create a perfect December.

Open the day, follow three tiny beats, and get back to real life a little more connected.
