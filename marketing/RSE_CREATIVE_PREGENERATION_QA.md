# RSE Creative Pre-Generation QA — Mandatory Gate

Status: LOCKED / MANDATORY
Owner: RSE Marketing Orchestrator
Applies to: ALL marketing images, carousels, reels, ads, A+ concepts, thumbnails, captions, hooks, CTAs and campaign copy.
Effective: 2026-09-30

## Non-negotiable rule

NO marketing image or marketing copy may be generated, approved, scheduled, or published until this checklist passes.

This file is a HARD PRE-FLIGHT GATE, not a style suggestion.

---

## 1. HAPPY MAKERS IDENTITY LOCK

Use only canonical Happy Makers.

Canonical team:
- MIMI — adult blonde woman, green eyes, long soft waves, warm natural expression.
- LULI — older girl, long straight black hair, warm composed smile, confident and smart.
- GRANDMA BIBI — grandmother, short auburn-brown hair, glasses, visibly older than the other women. Never youthify her.
- DILO — older boy. He must look visibly older and distinct from Alio.
- ALIO — younger boy. He must look visibly younger and distinct from Dilo.
- NINI — youngest girl, black hair in exactly TWO buns with long hair, bright warm eyes, small natural smile.

Character source rule:
- use the current owner-approved reference images / current Detective Academy locked appearance;
- never invent a substitute face;
- never use an old face variant just because it is convenient;
- never use one portrait for two different characters;
- never duplicate a character in the same composition unless the creative concept explicitly requires a mirrored/flashback treatment and owner approved it;
- before export, COUNT the characters and match every face to the intended name.

Specific Dilo / Alio gate:
- Dilo and Alio are NOT interchangeable;
- Dilo = older boy;
- Alio = younger boy;
- never reuse the same image, face crop or pose for both;
- if the distinction is not immediately obvious at thumbnail size, the asset fails QA.

Grandma Bibi gate:
- Bibi must clearly read as a grandmother;
- short auburn-brown hair + glasses + older facial proportions;
- no youthified face, teen-like skin treatment or same-age rendering as Mimi/Luli.

Nini gate:
- exactly two buns;
- never three buns/ponytails;
- do not babyify her;
- expression warm, smart, curious, not exaggerated.

---

## 2. NO DUPLICATES / NO VISUAL ACCIDENTS

Before export:
- no character appears twice by mistake;
- no two characters share the same face;
- no duplicated props, logos, question marks, badges or repeated decorative symbols unless they serve a clear story function;
- no extra people;
- no accidental missing character in a full-squad image;
- no mismatched hands, arms, object grips, or impossible overlaps;
- no wrong age relationship.

If a visual contains a named squad roster, every name must map to the correct unique character.

---

## 3. LANGUAGE QA — ZERO AI-SLOP

Every line must be:
- grammatically correct;
- natural English;
- clear on first read;
- logically connected to the image/product;
- understandable by a parent and, where intended, a child;
- free from vague coaching filler;
- free from meaningless sentence fragments;
- free from awkward slogan stacking.

Avoid generic AI phrases such as:
- unlock your potential;
- brighter kids;
- big impact;
- empower their journey;
- build stronger minds;
- endless possibilities;
- level up your life;
unless the line is tied to a precise, visible product mechanic.

Fragments are allowed only when deliberately functional, for example a 3-step UI sequence or a short button label.

No line should exist just because it "sounds inspirational".

---

## 4. COACHING / DEVELOPMENTAL COPY STANDARD

Marketing language must express a real, useful idea.

Preferred principles:
- confidence = knowing what to try next, not pretending to know;
- curiosity = asking a better question;
- resilience = looking again when the first idea fails;
- critical thinking = testing what the evidence supports;
- flexibility = changing your mind when the evidence changes;
- collaboration = different people notice different things;
- independence = support is available without taking over the decision.

Approved direction examples:
- "You don't need the answer yet. You need the next useful question."
- "Confidence is not being sure. It is knowing what to try next."
- "Good detectives change their minds when the evidence changes."
- "Getting stuck is part of the case. The skill is knowing what to try next."
- "Different minds notice different clues."
- "Your first idea is allowed to be wrong. Test it."
- "Don't guess. Find what the evidence can prove."
- "The book can guide the process. The child still makes the call."

Do NOT use "Same questions. Brighter kids." as a marketing slogan.
If it appears on an interior source page, treat it as printed source content only and do not amplify it in campaign copy.

---

## 5. PRODUCT-TRUTH GATE

Every marketing claim must be supported by the actual product.

For Detective Academy, verified mechanics include:
- Recruit Credential / call sign;
- reader joins the squad;
- 30 connected case files;
- one continuing Room Zero mystery;
- Witness Boards;
- Live Case Maps;
- visual evidence;
- codes, timelines, sequencing and spatial deduction;
- 3-Level Hint Vault;
- reasoning-first Solution Files;
- screen-free / pencil-and-paper use.

Never invent:
- fake puzzle pages;
- fake awards;
- fake reviews;
- fake included physical props;
- outcomes not supported by the product;
- neuroscience/psychology claims not supported by evidence.

---

## 6. HOOK STANDARD

The hook must answer one of these:
- Why should the parent stop scrolling?
- What problem does this solve?
- What surprising thing does the product do?
- What will the child want to do next?
- What belief does the creative overturn?

Good hook patterns:
- "If your child says 'I don't know' after 20 seconds, this is where the detective work starts."
- "The first case starts before Case 01."
- "One detective is still missing. Your child."
- "Getting stuck is not the end of the case."
- "A good detective is allowed to change their mind."
- "The answer is not the skill. Knowing what to check next is."

Avoid:
- 5 tips...
- Did you know...
- Boost...
- Unlock...
- generic motivational declarations.

---

## 7. CTA STANDARD

CTA must be:
- one action;
- clear;
- relevant to the content;
- natural for the platform;
- not repetitive;
- not salesy before trust is earned.

Examples:
- "Claim the Recruit Credential."
- "Choose their detective mode."
- "See how the Hint Vault works."
- "Open the first case."
- "Follow the evidence."
- "Meet the squad."
- "See inside the case file."
- Live only after verified listing URL: "View on Amazon."

---

## 8. STORY / LOGIC COHERENCE

Before generation or export ask:
1. What is the ONE idea of this creative?
2. What real parent/child need does it address?
3. What product proof supports it?
4. Is every sentence necessary?
5. Does the final CTA follow naturally from the idea?

If the creative tries to sell five ideas at once, simplify it.

---

## 9. VISUAL BRAND CHECK

Detective Academy:
- modern;
- premium;
- contemporary;
- navy / black / charcoal / warm controlled gold;
- no Victorian/Sherlock/steampunk styling;
- no fantasy castle feel;
- no random old library clutter;
- no overuse of crests/question marks/zeros.

Use real approved interior pages whenever the creative talks about a real product mechanic.

---

## 10. FINAL 10-POINT RELEASE CHECK

Before any creative is marked READY:
1. Correct Happy Makers only.
2. Character names and faces match.
3. Dilo and Alio are visibly different.
4. Grandma Bibi is visibly grandmother age.
5. No duplicate character or accidental repeat.
6. Grammar and punctuation checked.
7. Every line is clear, natural and useful.
8. Hook is strong and specific.
9. CTA is one clear next action.
10. Product proof is real and no unsupported claim is present.

Fail one = redo before owner review.
