## 3. BUYER PROBLEM

December can be wonderful and completely unreasonable at the same time.

The parent is already managing:
- school and work;
- practices / activities;
- shopping and gifts;
- food and hosting;
- errands and traffic;
- packages;
- screens and notifications;
- things somebody suddenly needs tomorrow;
- the pressure to make Christmas feel special.

The product must never sound like one more project.
