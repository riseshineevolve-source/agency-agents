## BOOK CHARACTER / TYPE
Hybrid middle-grade narrative adventure + detective puzzle book.
Thirty connected offline cases combine story, recurring mystery, logic puzzles, spatial witness boards, visual evidence, codes, timelines, routes and a final meta-deduction.

## TARGET AUDIENCE / AGE
Children ages 8–12.
Primary sweet spot: ages 9–11.
The low end must remain clear and readable; the high end must not feel babyish or school-workbook-like.

## DESIRED STYLE / VOICE / REGISTER
Premium middle-grade detective fiction embedded inside an activity/puzzle book.

The story must feel:
- fast, witty and highly readable;
- modern and grounded in real life rather than fantasy danger;
- clever without sounding like adults imitating youth slang;
- warm without coaching language;
- lightly sarcastic / teasing, never mean;
- logically airtight;
- surprising through fair-play reversals, callbacks and delayed payoffs;
- funny because of character behavior, timing and relationships, not random jokes.

Happy Makers are a real team/family:
- they tease one another because they know one another;
- they sometimes disagree;
- they support one another;
- they can be wrong;
- they let evidence beat ego;
- each has a recognizably different comic and investigative voice.

Reader arc:
RECRUIT -> expected teammate -> trusted detective -> FIELD SLOT 06.
By the end, the child should feel genuine affection for the team and feel that the Academy place was earned, not merely handed over.

## INTENDED EXPERIENCE
Offline sequential mystery experience.
Read and solve one case at a time, while preserving selected evidence for later callbacks.
The book should create strong “one more case” momentum but also allow natural stopping points.
The final five cases should feel like one sustained endgame built from work the reader actually completed earlier.
