# December Is Already Full. How Do You Find Time for Each Other?

By bedtime, you've reminded everyone about shoes, homework, dinner and whatever they need for school tomorrow. You've talked all day. Somehow, you haven't really had a moment together.

December doesn't cancel work, school or laundry. It just adds concerts, presents, parcels and a dozen things that suddenly have to happen before Christmas.

**Your family doesn't need one more big holiday activity. It might just need ten minutes when nobody is rushing anyone else.**

## Start with the evening you've got

After dinner, put the next job on hold for a few minutes. You don't need to announce a special family night. Try ten minutes together. If everyone only has five in them, five is enough.

That's the idea behind **Before Christmas Slips By**, a 24-day family Advent book from Rise.Shine.Evolve. and The Happy Makers. These are tiny glimpses from **three different days**, not a whole day given away.

### LET'S TAKE A MOMENT

Put down whatever is in your hands. The phone, the dish towel, the mug. Rest your empty hands on your knees. For one minute, nothing needs carrying, cleaning or putting away. It sounds almost silly. When was the last time your hands had nothing to do?

### LET'S DO THIS TOGETHER

One person makes up a ridiculous movement and sound. Someone else joins in with another. Before long, the living room has become a wonderfully useless **Family Machine**. The real challenge is getting the whole thing to stop at once. Good luck keeping a straight face.

### LET'S TALK

**“Who's done something helpful lately that almost nobody noticed?”**

Maybe someone made a snack, helped a younger sibling or remembered the thing everyone else forgot. Nobody needs to make a speech. Sometimes a simple, specific “I noticed. Thank you” is the part of the evening that stays with you.

## What if tonight just isn't the night?

Then skip it. Don't turn tomorrow into a catch-up session. The book includes two-person versions of the games, but nobody has to perform family togetherness on command. Use whatever time and energy you actually have.

**Before Christmas Slips By** gives families 24 ready-to-use days of short pauses, games and conversations, plus a small **TRY IT TOMORROW** idea. No extra shopping or elaborate activity prep.
