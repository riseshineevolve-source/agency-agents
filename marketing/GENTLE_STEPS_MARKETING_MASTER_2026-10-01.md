## Product truth

- 24-day Christmas / December family experience.
- Built for real families with real December schedules.
- Approximately ten minutes a day.
- Intended for families with children roughly 8–12, with adults participating.
- No elaborate preparation, craft project or extra shopping list required.
- Every day combines a short pause, a playable family activity and a brief conversation.
- Every PLAY has a two-person version.
- Happy Makers are warm, funny, character-specific guides.
- The product is about genuine shared time now, not merely manufacturing future memories.

## Parent problem

December is already full.

Parents do not need:
- another craft project;
- another perfect-family challenge;
- another complicated tradition;
- another thing to prepare.

They do want Christmas to feel like Christmas before the month disappears into logistics.
