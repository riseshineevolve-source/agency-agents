# E07 — The Spider Who Wove a Bottomless Conservatory (2026-10-12)

**Stage: actual SVG candidate V01 committed, ART QA PENDING. Not GOLD.** Owner asked for production, not a mere plan. Source is standalone two-ink SVG 2550 × 3300, viewBox 850 × 1100, with no embedded lettering or raster shading.

## Three genuinely different concept candidates (editorial assessment, not independent agent votes)

A. **Web as a bottomless glass well — SELECTED.** Precisely 90° overhead; a solitary eight-legged spider is physically anchored to a vast radial web whose concentric greenhouse glass panes recede into an apparent abyss. Floor at the bottom shows one impossible framed threshold. No familiar floor mirror. Optical mechanism is spatially aligned radial/ring foreshortening and warping, not generic wallpaper. Visual challenge: maintain enough leg/body clearance and large coloring sectors.

B. **Ceiling seen from under the spider** — underneath viewpoint in glass atrium; eight legs create different shadows on the panes. Reserve for another volume: not true overhead E07; duplicates E15 worm-eye.

C. **Spider as a negative-space blueprint** — a white spider silhouette emerges only when looking at several pieces of a conservatory roof simultaneously. Strong perceptual play, but high risk of losing a recognizable central spider, complicated to color. Reserve for experimental Volume 2.
