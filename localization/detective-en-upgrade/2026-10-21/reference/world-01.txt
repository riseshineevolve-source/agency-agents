THE NEURO-COACHING CONSOLE
■■
■■■ THE GLITCH ('The Hallway Explosion')
The Science: Trying to argue or fix problems the second you walk through
the door never works. If your body feels scratchy and mad, do the 'Wet
Dog Shake' on the doormat! Wiggle your arms and legs super fast for 10
seconds to shake the angry chemicals out of your muscles.
        THE BRAIN SECRET (The School Balloon)
The Science: When you are at school, you use a lot of brain energy to
follow rules, focus, and be polite. Psychologists call this 'Executive
Function.' It's like blowing air into a tight balloon all day. When you finally
reach your safe space (your home), your brain drops its guard and the
balloon pops! This is called 'Restraint Collapse.' 
In The Story: Alio and Nini were perfectly good all day, but they exploded
over a stuck zipper and a smelly bag the minute they walked in. They
weren't really mad at those things; their tired school balloons just popped!
THE TOOL ('The 10-Minute Turtle Shell')
The Science: A tired, overloaded brain cannot answer questions like 'How
was your day?'. It needs a sensory break. Deep pressure on your muscles
sends a signal to your nervous system that you are anchored and safe,
helping you calm down quickly. 
In The Story: Mimi didn't force them to talk. She put heavy cushions on
Alio and Nini's backs (The Turtle Shell) and gave them 10 minutes of quiet
Decompression Mode to reboot their systems.

 THE TOOL ('The Silent Sit')

slow down their racing heart.

 THE TOOL ('The 10-Minute Deal')
The Science: The hardest part of any task is starting. This is called
'Friction.' Your brain thinks the work will be painful and huge. But if you
make the task tiny, the brain says: 'Oh, I can do that!' 
In The Story: Dilo couldn't imagine practicing for an hour ('The sofa is 
 a black hole'). But when Mimi said 'Just do 10 minutes,' his brain stopped
being scared. And guess what? Once he started, he didn't want to stop!
       THE BRAIN SECRET (The Dopamine Trap)
The Science: Video games give you 'Fast Dopamine' (instant treats). Real
skills give you 'Slow Dopamine' (pride that comes later). Your brain is lazy.
It wants the fast treat! But the slow treat lasts much longer. 
In The Story: Mimi explained that games 'cheat' by giving gold coins
easily. Real life (soccer) is harder because the rewards (XP) are invisible
at first.
 THE GLITCH ('The Training Level')
The Science: When you make mistakes, you aren't failing. You are
building Myelin. That's a fancy word for brain-insulation. It makes your
thoughts faster.
In The Story: Dilo thought he was a 'confused penguin.' Mimi corrected
him: he was just in 'The Training Level.' Every time he missed the goal, his
brain was actually learning how to aim better next time.
