2. Several intros read like production notes rather than story beats.
3. Ordinary contact-result pages used `CASE CLOSED` even when the child had only established the next person to interview.
4. Case 03 had inherited wording implying an extra Room Zero mark on owner art, despite the current exact-ten owner-controlled photo mechanic using a neutral routed INTAKE-03 record.

7. Case 26 title `THE CASE OF THE MISSING PEOPLE` could imply actual missing persons even though the mechanic is empty-room extraction.
8. Several act transitions were informative but too report-like to sustain mystery momentum.
9. Room Zero explanation was logically sound but formatted like an FAQ rather than the emotional payoff of a 30-case investigation.
10. Some front-matter character copy could imply unsafe/incorrect Dilo behavior (e.g. “inventing facts”) instead of his locked character trait: overconfident naming/testing.
11. Bibi copy leaned unnecessarily on age as a joke rather than archive competence.
12. Some long sentences exceeded comfortable child-facing rhythm.
13. Repeated `YOUR MOVE: keep...` phrasing made Signal Log sections mechanical.
14. Hint Vault was useful but could better normalize using hints as legitimate detective tools.
15. Spatial-map rules used technical labels such as `Usable marked surfaces` instead of immediately child-readable map language.
