## Back matter function

1. One evening between Christmas and New Year:
   - revisit a few memories;
   - no meeting/workshop tone;
   - choose one thing worth keeping.

2. Happy Makers letter:
   - validate imperfect participation;
   - remind family this was not another December performance standard;
   - point to ordinary reuse of a few tools/games;
   - close warmly without creating a January obligation.

## Progression lock

The English edition must preserve the wave:

**EASY ENTRY -> FAMILY REALITY -> COOPERATION -> DEEPER NOTICING -> TEAM/IMPROV PEAKS -> BREATHER -> FINAL-WEEK RISE -> REPAIR -> CALLBACK FINALE**

Do not flatten this into 24 equal "mindfulness cards."
