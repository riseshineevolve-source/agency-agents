## Parent-facing caption system

### 01
Most puzzle books ask a child to solve the page.

Detective Academy gives them a place inside the investigation.

They join the Happy Makers squad and work through 30 connected case files built around maps, codes, clues and one hidden mystery.

A screen-free mystery adventure designed to feel like a case to enter, not another worksheet.

CTA:
**SAVE THIS FOR LAUNCH.**

### 02
Before Case 01 begins, the reader claims a Recruit Credential.

They choose their detective name, call sign and the skill they want to bring to the investigation. That small detail changes the role immediately: they are not watching the mystery. They are part of it.

CTA:
**SEE HOW THE INVESTIGATION STARTS.**

### 03
This is not 30 unrelated puzzles collected in one book.

The cases use maps, codes, visual evidence and logic, but they belong to one continuing investigation. Earlier details can matter again later, so the reader learns to keep the case files and follow the evidence.

CTA:
**FOLLOW THE EVIDENCE.**

### 04
A difficult case should not force a choice between giving up and reading the answer.

The Hint Vault gives support in three steps: first what to notice, then where to focus, then a stronger nudge. The reader still does the detective work.

The full Solution Files are separate when they are genuinely needed.

CTA:
**SEE HOW THE HINT VAULT WORKS.**
