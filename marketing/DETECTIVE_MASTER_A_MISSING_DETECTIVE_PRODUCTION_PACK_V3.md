## A1 — Reel / Short — 10–12 seconds

### 0.0–1.5s
Visual: five occupied identity/badge markers + one unmistakably empty sixth slot.

On-screen:
**ONE PLACE IS STILL EMPTY.**

Motion: immediate push-in. Something must move in the first second.

### 1.5–3.5s
A black envelope marked with one plain `0` slides into frame.

On-screen:
**A BLACK ENVELOPE ARRIVES.**

### 3.5–6.0s
Envelope opens to a clean blank Recruit Credential-style marketing card.

On-screen:
**DETECTIVE NAME: ______**

This is a marketing composition, not a fake photographed interior page.

### 6.0–8.5s
Fast category beats:
**MAPS**
**CODES**
**VISUAL EVIDENCE**
**LOGIC**

Do not show real answers, solution maps or Room Zero clues.

### 8.5–12.0s
End card:
**30 CASES. ONE HIDDEN MYSTERY.**
**YOU'RE THE MISSING DETECTIVE.**

CTA:
**FOLLOW. YOUR FIRST CASE IS COMING.**

### Optional voice-over
"One place on the squad is still empty. A black envelope arrives. Thirty cases lead into one hidden mystery. The last detective? That's you."

Sound:
envelope slide + restrained scanner click + paper snap + subtle Academy pulse.
No horror sting. No police siren.
