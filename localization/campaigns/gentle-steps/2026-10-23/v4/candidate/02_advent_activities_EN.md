# An Activity Advent Calendar Where Parents Get to Play Too

The children are asleep. You remember tomorrow's Advent envelope is supposed to contain an activity. What activity? And do you have everything you need for it?

Some families love making their own calendars. If that's you, enjoy it. If you've reached December with plenty to do already, it's useful to have the ideas waiting on the page.

**Before Christmas Slips By**, from Rise.Shine.Evolve. and The Happy Makers, gives families 24 ready-to-use days of short pauses, games and conversation. About ten minutes a day, with no extra shopping or activity prep.

The grown-ups get a turn as well. You could be the one trying to guess what on earth everyone is acting out, instead of the one finding the scissors and explaining the rules for the third time.

## A look at the activities

Here are three short examples from **three different days**. They're a taste of the book, rather than a complete day's page.

### LET’S TAKE A MOMENT

**One Sound Quieter.** The TV is on. Notifications keep chiming. Someone's trying to tell a story. Turn down one source of noise you can control, such as the TV, for a minute. Listen to the room with a little less going on.

### LET’S DO THIS TOGETHER

**Reverse Charades.** One person will be guessing. The others quietly choose a shared prompt, then act it out at once, without speaking or agreeing on the gestures. With "building a snowman", three people may appear to be playing three different winter sports. That's part of the fun.

### LET’S TALK

**“What little things have we picked up from each other?”**

A phrase your mum says. A song your child got stuck in everyone's head. The way Grandad makes a sandwich. Which small habit have you borrowed, and from whom? You might even hear a favourite expression delivered in a very familiar tone.

## Ready for a weeknight

The games include **TWO PEOPLE** versions, so a smaller group can play too. **TRY IT TOMORROW** offers a small way to use that day's pause again. Some evenings you may want all three parts; on others, one will do.

You can skip a day without creating a backlog. Anyone can sit a game out. And whoever opens the book doesn't have to spend the evening running the entertainment.

Have a look through the activities and choose something you'd like to join in with. The grown-ups are allowed to be every bit as ridiculous as everyone else.
