# TARGET_LANGUAGE_AND_STYLE_PROFILE — Detective Academy PL V02

Route: **A / SAME_LANGUAGE_PREMIUM_UPGRADE**
Target language: **Polish (pl-PL)**
Audience: **children roughly 8–12**
Product: premium connected detective adventure, not a school worksheet.

## Reader experience target

The child should feel:
1. recruited into a real investigative squad;
2. increasingly competent without being patronized;
3. that the physical book itself remembers prior evidence;
4. that Happy Makers are people they like, not exposition machines;
5. that every case is fair and solvable;
6. that the finale rewards work already done rather than springing a new trick.

## Polish register

- contemporary native Polish;
- professionally edited book prose;
- intelligent and lively, never babyish;
- warm, smart, lightly irreverent;
- dialogue may be more colloquial than narration;
- instructions must remain complete and precise;
- no translationese, generic coaching voice, therapy register, TikTok imitation, bureaucratic police/legal jargon or social-media cadence;
- no English-shaped sentence hinges;
- no em-dash used as a substitute for Polish syntax in running prose;
- Polish quotation marks where quotation marks are needed.

## Golden writing test

Final question for every line:
**„Czy dobry polski autor napisałby to właśnie tak, gdyby nie widział wersji angielskiej?”**

For humor:
**„Czy dowcip działa po polsku sam z siebie, a nie dlatego, że rozumiemy angielski mechanizm?”**

For puzzles:
**„Czy po zmianie polskiego zdania zbiór możliwych rozwiązań jest dokładnie ten sam?”**

## Character voice matrix

- **Mimi:** practical organizer, quick to turn chaos into next action; dry competence; occasional everyday self-own.
- **Luli:** precision, skepticism, deadpan corrections; strongest anti-nonsense filter.
- **Dilo:** tech/pattern enthusiasm, anthropomorphizes systems and objects, comic overengineering; logic still wins.
- **Nini:** concrete observation, people/emotion sensitivity, small literal twists; never baby talk.
- **Alio:** routes, dramatic theories, shortcuts, helmets and overconfident improvisation; lovable, not incompetent.
- **Bibi:** warm, sharp, experienced archive mentor; dry authority, never caricatured as old-fashioned.

No two characters should become interchangeable generic witty voices.

## Gender-neutral child address

Do not use slash forms or parenthetical gender. Avoid direct forms that unnecessarily force grammatical gender. Restructure naturally.

## Humor standard

Humor should:
- come from character and situation;
- reduce pressure without weakening stakes;
- never hide a clue or alter logic;
- use callbacks sparingly;
- avoid one joke mechanism dominating every case.
