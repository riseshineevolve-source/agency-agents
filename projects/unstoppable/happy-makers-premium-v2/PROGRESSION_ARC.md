# Project Unstoppable V2 — 31-Day Journey Arc

## Product promise
31 days. Six Happy Makers. One real life that has to work.

Every day keeps the same six-part rhythm:
INTRO → BRIEFING → WORKBENCH → SYSTEM REBOOT → GLITCH & PUZZLE → FREE ROAM.

What changes is the level of independence, combination and evidence.

## STAGE 1 — KNOW YOUR CONTROLS (Days 1–7)
Goal: establish agency, self-knowledge, body signals, people/safety map, communication and reset.
Reader result by Day 7:
- 3-value compass;
- trait-in-context cards;
- body-signal dashboard;
- support/squad map;
- response plan for mean comments;
- conversation decoder;
- 3-part reset.
Checkpoint Day 7: choose two tools that already feel useful and one situation for each.

## STAGE 2 — RUN YOUR SYSTEM (Days 8–14)
Goal: learning, attention, starting, project breakdown, mornings, distraction design, review.
Reader result by Day 14:
- recall method;
- focus block;
- five-minute start;
- project map;
- morning friction fix;
- focus rule;
- first two-week evidence review.
Checkpoint Day 14: combine two tools in one real 30-minute test and record what happened.

## STAGE 3 — HANDLE REAL LIFE (Days 15–21)
Goal: overload, money choices, budgeting, responsibility, calendar, boundaries, effectiveness.
Reader result by Day 21:
- SOS sort;
- price pause;
- NOW/LATER/FLEX plan;
- 5-minute environment reset;
- four-layer week;
- three NO scripts;
- effort/usefulness filter.
Checkpoint Day 21: take one messy real-life situation and use two tools in sequence.

## STAGE 4 — BUILD FORWARD (Days 22–27)
Goal: strengths evidence, direction, goals, setbacks, advice, contribution.
Reader result by Day 27:
- strengths evidence map;
- direction board;
- usable goal card;
- retry report;
- advice filter;
- tiny impact mission.
Checkpoint Day 27: show one piece of evidence that a tool changed an action, decision or result.

## STAGE 5 — MAKE IT YOURS (Days 28–31)
Goal: character-in-action, noticing good without denial, evidence audit, personal tool kit and reset.
Reader result:
- three character words with behaviors;
- five good receipts;
- Keep/Drop/Changed/Next audit;
- personal Unstoppable Plan;
- final RESET key.

## Difficulty model
Not "every day harder".
BUILD → BREATHE → BUILD → PEAK → CLOSE.

Escalation:
1. notice;
2. name;
3. choose;
4. test;
5. compare evidence;
6. combine tools;
7. use independently;
8. create personal system.

## Daily design law
Every day must answer:
- What concrete thing does the reader leave with?
- What can they actually try outside the book?
- What evidence could tell them whether it helped?
- Why is this day here *now* in the 31-day arc?
- Which Happy Maker is best suited to lead it?
