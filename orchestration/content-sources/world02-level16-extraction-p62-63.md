## p62 — THE NEURO-COACHING CONSOLE

### // FILE: 046 — THE BRAIN SECRET (Theory of Mind)

**The Science:** 'Theory of Mind' is the ability to understand that other people have different information, thoughts, and feelings than you. Kids (and angry adults) often forget this. They think: 'If I see a 6, everyone sees a 6. If they say 9, they are lying!'

**In The Story:** Dilo saw a black cord from his angle. Luli saw a grey one. They didn't have the same data, but they both thought the other was lying.

### // FILE: 047 — THE TOOL ('The Swap-Goggles')

**The Science:** You cannot solve a fight from your own shoes. You must physically or mentally move your position to see their data.

**In The Story:** Mimi physically moved Luli to Dilo's side of the paper.

Step 1: Stop talking.

Step 2: Ask 'What does it look like from YOUR side?'

Step 3: Listen like a detective, not a lawyer.

### // FILE: 048 — THE GLITCH ('You did it on purpose!')

**The Science:** 'Hostile Attribution Bias'. We have a glitch where we assume accidents are actually attacks.

**In The Story:** Dilo assumed Luli unplugged his game on purpose as revenge for the yogurt.

**Correction:** Assume positive intent (or at least, neutral intent). Most people aren't plotting against you; they are just trying to plug in a toaster.

### QUEST — TALK ABOUT IT

**In The Story:** Dilo and Luli were 100% sure they were right, but they were both wrong because Nini did it!

**Your Turn:** Have you ever had a fight with a friend or sibling where you both thought you were right? What happened when you found out the whole truth?

### // FILE: BRAIN_HACK — TOP SECRET SCIENCE (For Big and Small Brains)

Did you know that "Theory of Mind" is a skill that develops slowly over years? A 3-year-old physically cannot understand that you don't know what they are thinking. By the time you are 8, your brain is getting good at it, but when the Amygdala (Lizard Brain) takes over during a fight, that advanced skill shuts down completely! You temporarily lose the ability to see things from another perspective until you calm down.
