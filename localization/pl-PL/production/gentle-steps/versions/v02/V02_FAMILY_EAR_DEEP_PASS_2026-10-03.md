## Review axes

1. Child pull (roughly 8-12): the activity must sound worth trying, not like a lesson disguised as fun.
2. Older-child anti-cringe: avoid preschool energy, fake youth slang, compulsory emotional display and workshop language.
3. Tired-parent usability: fast start, little or no preparation, clear mechanics.
4. Humor: Happy Makers comments need an actual comic mechanism and distinct character voice.
5. Depth: humor may release pressure, but must not flatten appreciation, apology, memory, gratitude, dreams or the final reflection.

## Research signals

Fresh Polish family/game research supports quick start, clear rules, equal footing between children and adults, simple rules with a real challenge, no-prop or almost-no-prep play, and short rounds.

## Targeted fixes applied

### Day 7
PODAJ DALEJ, BEZ SŁÓW -> REAKCJA ŁAŃCUCHOWA.
The same mechanics now read as a nonverbal signal challenge rather than a preschool-style affection exercise.

### Day 9
MAŁY PLUS DNIA -> CO DZIŚ URATOWAŁO DZIEŃ?.
This separates the talk section from the breathing/gratitude section on the same day and gives it a stronger ordinary-life hook.

### Day 10
NASZE HASŁO -> ZDANIE, KTÓRE DO NAS PASUJE.
The prompt now rejects ceremonial slogans and asks for a sentence, rule, shortcut or family phrase that genuinely feels theirs.

### Day 17
CO DAJE NAM ENERGIĘ? -> KIEDY MAMY NAJWIĘCEJ ENERGII?.
This removes coaching-style phrasing while preserving the source function.

### Day 18
JEDNO SŁOWO, ALE TRAFIONE -> SŁOWO, KTÓRE DO CIEBIE PASUJE.
The prompt lands more naturally in Polish while preserving the one-word appreciation mechanic.

### Day 22
Reflective sentence starters were rewritten into more natural Polish, including:
- Najbardziej sobą jestem, kiedy...
- Dziś mogę o sobie powiedzieć, że...
- Coś ważnego dla mnie, czego nie widać od razu, to...
- Kiedy myślę «dom», pierwsze słowo, które przychodzi mi do głowy, to...
