# TARGET LANGUAGE AND STYLE PROFILE

Book: Happy Makers Detective Academy — The Mystery of Room Zero — Book 1
Route: A — SAME_LANGUAGE_PREMIUM_UPGRADE
Target language: English
Audience: ages 8–12
Type: narrative detective / puzzle adventure

## Desired reader experience

The reader should feel recruited into the Happy Makers team, not spoken to from outside it.
By the middle of the book, the reader should feel like the sixth chair at the Case Table:
trusted, teased lightly, expected to think, and treated as capable.

The voice should be:
- modern, natural English;
- premium book prose, not social-media copy;
- funny without performing youth slang;
- occasionally dry, lightly sassy and character-specific;
- warm without becoming therapy/coaching language;
- fast enough for 8–12, but never babyish;
- clear enough for puzzle instructions;
- respectful of the reader's intelligence.

## Character voice locks

MIMI — practical captain; concise, competent, dry when needed; quietly protective of the method.
LULI — precise, skeptical, sharp; funniest when a single dry correction punctures nonsense.
DILO — tech/code enthusiast; dramatic naming, systems, overconfidence, competitive energy; jokes should be quick.
ALIO — routes/recon; physical-adventure imagination, helmet callback, confident theories; good-natured chaos.
NINI — people/courage; warm but not sentimental, perceptive, understated wit, notices social reality and quiet evidence.
BIBI — archive mentor; deadpan authority, experience, factual discipline; can be gently ruthless about bad reasoning.

## Humor rules

- Keep jokes short and situational.
- Prefer character clash over standalone punchlines.
- Light sarcasm is allowed when it targets a silly idea, object, system or friend-group dynamic — never a sincere feeling or failure.
- Avoid forced meme language, TikTok cadence and adult-written teen slang.
- Avoid stacking three jokes where one lands.
- Keep recurring motifs (helmet, biscuits, Dilo's systems) only when they still earn the callback.

## Content locks

Do not change:
- 30-case order and titles unless explicitly listed as an approved wording fix;
- puzzle mechanics, witness truth, coordinates, answers, Room Zero chain or meta extraction;
- Case 03 exact ten differences;
- Case 05 final sequence BALL–STAR–BOLT–HEART–KEY–MOON;
- Case 21 message THE ANSWER IS IN WHAT YOU LEAVE EMPTY;
- Case 26 extraction CHECK THE OLD MAP;
- Rule Zero: ZERO ASSUMPTIONS. NOTICE FIRST. THEORIZE SECOND.;
- Case 29 D3;
- Bibi route-note authorship reveal;
- Field slot 06 / reader certification payoff;
- Archive File 001 triangle callback;
- Hint/Solution truth.

## Editing law

KEEP beats rewrite.
Only change a segment for a documented reader-experience, logic, clarity, rhythm, humor, voice or repetition reason.
