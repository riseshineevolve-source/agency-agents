1. THE PICK: Choose 3 values that matter to you right now.
Try: honesty, loyalty, humor, freedom, curiosity, kindness,
courage, creativity, fairness, independence. Add your own.

notification, channel, or feed you do not actually want.

DAY 2: STRENGTHS & GLITCHES
[Luli CONNECTING...]
A trait is not a permanent score. Stubborn can help when
you're finishing something hard and cause problems when
you stop listening. Quiet can mean unsure in one moment
and observant in another.

better move.
A label is a terrible user manual.
“
A TRAIT CAN FEEL LIKE A BUG IN ONE SITUATION

MAIN QUEST: The Context Switch

question.
