## Owner feedback converted into book-wide rules

1. December must feel real, not decorative.
2. Quiet moments must solve a believable micro-problem inside a busy day.
3. Games must be fun first, with cooperation, coordination, support or trust built into the mechanics.
4. Conversation must reveal something real about family members without sounding like therapy cards.
5. Happy Makers comments must be memorable enough to become family callbacks.
6. No fake youth slang, no preschool energy, no corporate jokes.
7. Emotional depth stays. Humor lowers pressure, it does not flatten the moment.
8. The full daily experience should remain feasible in roughly ten minutes.

## Research integration

Current external evidence used directionally, not as a claim inside the book:
- Polish 2025 research documents strong pre-Christmas rush/stress.
- Polish 2025 family-conversation research shows very short daily windows for meaningful parent-child conversation.
- Polish holiday-conversation research shows demand for deeper, less superficial family conversation.
- Research on children 7–12 supports energetic group games and structured cooperation as useful social/cooperative contexts.
- Pediatric breathing literature is promising but heterogeneous, therefore V02 uses modest language and no medical promises.
