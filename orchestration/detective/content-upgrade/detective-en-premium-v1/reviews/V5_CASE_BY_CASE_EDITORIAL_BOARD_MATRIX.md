## Board-wide conclusion

The book's **twist architecture is stronger than its scene architecture**.

The next improvement should NOT invent more twists.

It should:
1. dramatize the strongest existing causal beats;
2. make every requested deduction visibly useful;
3. make ordinary cases feel like lived Academy incidents;
4. vary the emotional job of spatial cases;
5. make the reader's prior work visibly change what happens next;
6. protect all fair-play seeds/payoffs.
