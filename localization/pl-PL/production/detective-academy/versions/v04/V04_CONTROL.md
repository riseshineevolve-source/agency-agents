## V04 owner language lock

### 1. NO "DRUŻYNA"
The active reader-facing text must contain zero forms of the word "drużyna".
Choose the natural solution by context:
- Happy Makers;
- ekipa;
- zespół;
- wszyscy;
- reszta;
- they/omit the noun when Polish does not need it.

Do not mechanically replace every instance with "ekipa".

### 2. POLISH FIRST, SOURCE SECOND
Write the sentence a good Polish children's author would naturally write.
Then verify it still carries the source function and puzzle truth.
Never preserve awkward source structure for visual similarity to English.

### 3. NO BUREAUCRATIC TRANSLATIONESE
Avoid reader-facing phrases such as:
- stanowisko rekrutacyjne;
- finalna klasyfikacja;
- obszar mapy, when "to samo pomieszczenie" is what is meant;
- rekord when "zapis" or "wpis" is natural;
- legalistic wording when simpler Polish works;
- process vocabulary in narrative unless a literal Academy computer/system is the subject.

### 4. NATURAL CHILD/FAMILY POLISH
Target age 8–12.
Bookish but easy.
No fake youth slang.
No teacher/workshop voice.
No corporate-coaching phrasing.
No over-clever metaphors that slow a puzzle.
No sentence should require the reader to reverse-engineer an English original.

### 5. HUMOR
Rebuild jokes for Polish timing.
Short, situational, character-specific.
If a literal source joke is flat in Polish, replace the joke while preserving scene function and facts.

### 6. HEADINGS MAY BE RE-AUTHORED
Headings can change if the Polish version becomes clearer/more natural and puzzle identity is preserved.
Avoid translated slogans.
