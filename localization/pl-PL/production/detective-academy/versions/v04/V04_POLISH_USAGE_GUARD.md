# V04 Polish Usage Guard

## Global banned / avoid list
Reader-facing active text:
- all forms of "drużyna" — banned globally by owner;
- "stanowisko rekrutacyjne" — banned;
- "wchodzisz do gry" — avoid unless a literal game is meant;
- "pierwszy odruch" as translated motivational framing — avoid;
- "finalna klasyfikacja" — prefer plain task-specific wording;
- "rekord" for a document/log entry — prefer "wpis", "zapis" or "rejestr" as context requires;
- "obszar mapy" when the actual meaning is room/zone/place — choose a concrete noun.

## Do not overcorrect
Words such as "teoria", "dowód", "rejestr", "system", "formularz" are not banned. They are appropriate when they name a real detective concept, record or machine. The problem is translated syntax, not vocabulary by itself.

## Preferred narrative texture
- concrete action;
- ordinary verbs;
- fewer abstract nouns;
- less process language;
- varied sentence length;
- readable aloud;
- short character-specific humor;
- no decorative slang.
