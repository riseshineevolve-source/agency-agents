## Owner direction

V02 is directionally strong and must not be broadly rewritten for novelty.

V03 focuses on four material upgrade areas:

1. **Game system**
   - curate around four functional families:
     - TEAM / cooperation / trust / shared success,
     - LAUGHTER / genuinely funny without clowning or forced performance,
     - WORD + IMPRO / verbal flexibility, wit, questions, spontaneous thinking,
     - OTHER BEST-OF / only strong activities worth keeping;
   - games should feel appealing enough that a family actually wants to join;
   - no elimination, humiliation, forced silliness or fake bonding;
   - instructions must be immediately runnable.

2. **Instruction clarity**
   - every game must make clear who starts, what happens first, turn order, rounds, ending and reset/error rule;
   - two-person version must be explicit whenever the normal group mechanic changes;
   - safety/consent remain first-class rules.

3. **ZWOLNIJ -> practical toolbox**
   - every day adds one short **NA JUTRO** application sentence;
   - the evening exercise teaches a tool that can be reused next day in a concrete situation: rush, test, conflict, overload, screens, waiting, frustration, tiredness, noise or unfinished tasks;
   - no therapeutic promises, mindfulness jargon or quasi-clinical claims.

4. **Happy Makers comment precision**
   - preserve comments that land naturally;
   - remove jokes that sound written merely because a joke slot exists;
   - humor must follow the concrete scene and character voice.

Owner golden example:
**Mimi: Puste ręce. Niczego przez minutę nie odkładamy na właściwe miejsce. Korzystajcie!**

Owner defect example:
**Mimi: Dom to też miejsce, w którym najgorsze skarpetki świata mają pełne prawa obywatelskie.**
The issue is not joke intensity but semantic fit: socks are an arbitrary object for the intended "home = permission to be comfortable / keep beloved old things" idea.
