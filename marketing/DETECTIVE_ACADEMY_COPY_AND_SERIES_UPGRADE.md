## Core positioning

Do not sell generic "brain benefits".
Sell a child entering a real-feeling investigation and learning how to think through uncertainty.

Parent need:
"I want something my child will genuinely engage with that also develops useful thinking habits without feeling like homework."

Child need:
"I want a role, a mystery, a squad, and a reason to keep going."

## New campaign thought

**THE SKILL IS NOT KNOWING. THE SKILL IS KNOWING WHAT TO TRY NEXT.**

This is the developmental spine of the campaign.

## Strong coaching lines

Use selectively, one per creative.

1. **You don't need the answer yet. You need the next useful question.**
2. **Confidence is not being sure. It is knowing what to try next.**
3. **Good detectives change their minds when the evidence changes.**
4. **Getting stuck is part of the case. The skill is knowing what to try next.**
5. **Your first idea is allowed to be wrong. Test it.**
6. **Don't guess. Find what the evidence can prove.**
7. **Different minds notice different clues.**
8. **The book can guide the process. The child still makes the call.**
9. **A clue is useful only if you know what question it answers.**
10. **Look again before you give up. That is often where the case changes.**

## Upgraded signature series

### SERIES 01 — THE NEXT USEFUL QUESTION
Parent/child insight:
Children often stop at "I don't know."

Hook:
**"I don't know" is not the end of the case.**

Payoff:
**You don't need the answer yet. You need the next useful question.**

Product proof:
Case objective + evidence + Hint Vault.

CTA:
**See what they try next.**

---

### SERIES 02 — CHANGE YOUR MIND
Insight:
Changing an answer after new evidence is strength, not failure.

Hook:
**The smartest detective in the room might be the one who says, "I was wrong."**

Payoff:
**Good detectives change their minds when the evidence changes.**

Product proof:
Witness statement / evidence comparison / reasoning solution.

CTA:
**Follow the evidence.**

---

### SERIES 03 — STUCK IS PART OF THE CASE
Insight:
Parents want support without handing over the answer.

Hook:
**What happens after "I can't do it"?**

Payoff:
**Getting stuck is part of the case. The skill is knowing what to try next.**

Product proof:
3-Level Hint Vault.

CTA:
**See how the Hint Vault works.**

---

### SERIES 04 — DIFFERENT MINDS, DIFFERENT CLUES
Insight:
Not every child contributes in the same way.

Hook:
**One child spots the detail. Another asks the question that changes everything.**

Payoff:
**Different minds notice different clues.**

Character lock:
Use exactly one unique portrait per:
- Mimi
- Luli
- Grandma Bibi
- Dilo
- Alio
- Nini
No duplicated face/crop.

CTA:
**Which detective mode sounds like your child?**

Suggested labels:
- MIMI — turns a mess into the next useful step.
- LULI — spots contradictions and checks the small print.
- DILO — tests ideas and loves codes.
- ALIO — notices routes and unexpected physical details.
- NINI — notices feelings and details other people skip.
- GRANDMA BIBI — remembers what matters and knows what to verify.

---

### SERIES 05 — THE READER MAKES THE CALL
Insight:
Agency matters more than passive worksheet completion.

Hook:
**The book gives the evidence. Your child makes the call.**

Payoff:
**Support stays available. Ownership stays with the reader.**

Product proof:
Recruit Credential + case verdict + Hint Vault.

CTA:
**Claim the Recruit Credential.**

---

### SERIES 06 — BEFORE CASE 01
Hook:
**The first case starts before Case 01.**

Payoff:
The child chooses:
- Detective Name
- Official Call Sign
- Best Detective Skill

Strong line:
**Before they solve a case, they decide what kind of detective they want to be.**

CTA:
**Choose the call sign.**

---

### SERIES 07 — DON'T GUESS
Hook:
**A mystery is not a guessing game.**

Payoff:
**Don't guess. Find what the evidence can prove.**

Product proof:
Witness Board / Live Case Map / Solution Files.

CTA:
**Open the case file.**

---

### SERIES 08 — CONFIDENCE WITHOUT THE SPEECH
Hook:
**Confidence is not "I know."**

Payoff:
**It is "I can work out what to try next."**

Parent angle:
No motivational lecture. Give the child a structure that lets them keep moving.

CTA:
**See how a case guides them.**

## Copy that is retired from marketing

Do not use as campaign slogans:
- Same questions. Brighter kids.
- Curiosity makes brighter kids.
- Kinder humans. Brighter tomorrows.
- Real skills. Real impact.
- A brighter tomorrow solves more.
- Unlock your potential.
- Big results.
- Endless possibilities.

Reason:
too generic, too vague, too AI-like, or not tightly tied to product truth.

## Final creative rule

Every Detective Academy asset should make the viewer understand one of these within 2 seconds:
- the child becomes part of the story;
- the mystery is connected;
- the child has a next move when stuck;
- evidence matters more than guessing;
- different strengths belong in the same team.

If it does not, the creative is decorative rather than persuasive.
