# Gentle Steps PL V03 — Game System Audit

Primary design rule: every game must earn its place. "Different" is not enough.

## Four primary game families

### 1. TEAM
Shared goal, interdependence, coordination, trust, communication or leaderless cooperation.
No lecture about teamwork. The mechanic itself creates it.

### 2. LAUGHTER
The situation creates humor naturally.
No humiliation, forced dancing, babyish clowning or "be silly now" requirement.

### 3. WORD + IMPRO
Fast verbal thinking, association, questions, storytelling, explanation, tone and improvisation.
This family is intentionally prominent because it works well for ages 8–12 and adults together.

### 4. OTHER BEST-OF
Music, observation, ritual and other activities that do not fit the first three groups but are strong enough to keep.

## Design gates

Every retained/rebuilt game must pass:
- 3–6 minute core loop;
- near-zero setup;
- no special materials;
- no elimination;
- low-stakes failure;
- clear start;
- clear turn order;
- clear finish;
- explicit two-person version if the group mechanic changes;
- no unsafe speed;
- no forced touch;
- no forced embarrassment;
- fun is produced by the mechanic, not by telling the reader to "be funny".
