## Main conversion blockers

1. BELIEF GAP
"Is this just another generic puzzle book?"

Answer:
show the dossier format, book-long mystery, connected cases, Hint Vault and reasoning solutions.

2. OWNERSHIP GAP
"Will my child actually want to do it?"

Answer:
show the moment of receiving the blank detective badge / opening the case file / writing a detective name / solving a real case.

3. FRICTION GAP
"Will I have to teach or supervise everything?"

Answer:
show self-directed case progression, optional hints, reasoning solutions and pencil-ready structure without claiming total independence for every child.

4. CHOICE GAP
"Why this instead of another puzzle/activity book?"

Answer:
30 cases are part of one mystery; old files can matter again; the reader has a role inside the story.

5. TIMING GAP
Autumn:
screen-free back-to-routine challenge.

Holiday:
gift an adventure.

Black Friday:
value depth before any owner-approved discount.
