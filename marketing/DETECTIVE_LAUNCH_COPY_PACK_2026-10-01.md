## Parent-first captions

### Missing detective
Most activity books ask a child to solve the puzzle.

Detective Academy gives them a place inside the investigation.

Before Case 01, they claim a Recruit Credential, choose a detective name and create an official call sign.

Then the cases begin.

30 connected case files.
One continuing mystery.
One open place on the squad.

ONE DETECTIVE IS STILL MISSING.
YOUR CHILD.

Prelink CTA:
SAVE THIS FOR LAUNCH.

Live CTA:
JOIN THE ACADEMY.

### Don't guess
A clue is not an answer.

A map is not decoration.

A witness statement is not something to skim.

Detective Academy asks children to slow down, compare the evidence and make a call they can actually support.

DON'T GUESS.
PROVE IT.

Prelink CTA:
SEE HOW A CASE WORKS.

### Different minds
One child notices the route.
Another catches the contradiction.
Another asks the question everyone else skipped.

That is the point.

The Happy Makers do not all think the same way - and the reader does not have to either.

DIFFERENT MINDS.
ONE STRONG TEAM.

### Getting stuck
Getting stuck is part of detective work.

The skill is knowing what to try next.

Detective Academy includes a 3-Level Hint Vault:
Level 1 - what to notice.
Level 2 - where to focus.
Level 3 - a stronger nudge.

The full solution is there when the reader wants it - not before.

### The book remembers
A case can be solved and still matter later.

That is why the book keeps asking the reader to save certain evidence, maps and records.

KEEP YOUR CASE FILES.

Some details make more sense after the investigation grows.

No spoilers.
