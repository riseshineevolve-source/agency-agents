# December Is Full. How Do You Make Time for Each Other?

By bedtime, you’ve reminded everyone about shoes, homework, dinner and whatever they need for school tomorrow. You’ve talked all day. Somehow, you haven’t had time to hear what made anyone laugh.

December doesn’t cancel work, school or laundry. It adds concerts, presents and a dozen things that suddenly have to happen before Christmas.

**You’d like to enjoy a little of December together, too.**

## Ten minutes when nobody’s rushing anyone

After dinner, let the next job wait. Sit down together and open a book.

That’s the idea behind **Before Christmas Slips By**, a 24-day family Advent calendar from Rise.Shine.Evolve. and The Happy Makers, with short pauses, games and conversations. Here are glimpses from **three different days**.

### LET’S TAKE A MOMENT

**Empty Hands.** Put the phone, mug or dish towel somewhere safe. Rest your hands on your knees. For a minute, there’s nothing to carry, clean or put away. When did your hands last have absolutely nothing to do?

### LET’S DO THIS TOGETHER

In the **Family Machine**, everyone adds their own movement and sound. Soon the living room sounds like a factory nobody knows how to switch off. Now try stopping all at once. Without agreeing on a signal.

### LET’S TALK

**“Who’s done something helpful lately that almost nobody noticed?”**

Someone helped a younger sibling. Someone remembered the thing everyone else forgot. A simple “I noticed. Thank you” can change the feel of an ordinary evening.

## What if tonight isn’t the night?

Skip it. Tomorrow doesn’t need a double session. The games include two-person versions, and **TRY IT TOMORROW** suggests how to use that short pause again tomorrow. There’s no extra shopping or activity prep.

Sometimes the evening you remember is the one with the ridiculous noises and an unexpected thank-you.
