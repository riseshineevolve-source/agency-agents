### Case-world continuity
The Academy is a working investigation hub across multiple days/weeks, not a corridor in which thirty random incidents occur.

Case origins intentionally vary:
- intake printer / dormant Academy system;
- Academy staff or mentors;
- events inside specialist Academy wings;
- problems Happy Makers witness directly;
- nearby partner venues / field exercises;
- direct continuation from the preceding case.

Every case intro must answer:
1. why this file/problem appears NOW;
2. what happened;
3. why this exact deduction matters;
4. how it connects to the ongoing investigation.
