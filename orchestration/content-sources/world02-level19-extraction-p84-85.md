## p84 — THE NEURO-COACHING CONSOLE

### // FILE: 055 — THE BRAIN SECRET (Prediction Error)

**The Science:** Kids often have 'Sticky Brains' (Cognitive Rigidity). Your brain loves to predict the future. When reality doesn't match the plan, your brain panics and throws an Error Code! It feels physically painful.

**In The Story:** Alio reacted like the world was ending because his brain predicted waterslides, and reality delivered thunder.

### // FILE: 056 — THE TOOL ('The Pivot')

**The Science:** Teach your brain that "Change does not equal Danger".

**In The Story:** Use the basketball metaphor. When a defender blocks you, you don't cry and sit on the court. You PIVOT (keep one foot planted, move the other) to find a new path around them!

### // FILE: 057 — THE GLITCH ('You promised!')

**The Science:** To a child's brain, a 'Plan' feels like a blood oath.

**In The Story:** Alio called Mimi a liar because the weather changed.

**Correction:** A plan is an intention. Validate your disappointment ('My bucket is broken'), but remember your Captain cannot control the weather!

### QUEST — TALK ABOUT IT

**In The Story:** Alio flapped on the floor like a seal because his plan was ruined.

**Your Turn:** What is the hardest part for you when an exciting plan gets cancelled? Do you get angry, sad, or do you want to hide?

### // FILE: BRAIN_HACK — TOP SECRET SCIENCE (For Big and Small Brains)

Why did Mimi start jumping and shaking? When you face a massive disappointment, your body shoots adrenaline into your muscles, preparing you to fight. Doing a "Shake-Out Dance" physically burns off the stress chemicals, sending a message back to the brain that you are safe and ready to Pivot.
