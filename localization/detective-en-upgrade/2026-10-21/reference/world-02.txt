■ THE BRAIN SECRET (Habit Stacking)
The Science: Your brain is incredibly smart, but it can be very lazy! It wants to
save battery. Every time you argue with yourself ('Should I brush my teeth?'),
you burn your 'Willpower Fuel'. If you chain habits together (Pajamas -> Brush),
your brain goes into autopilot and just executes the script. 
In The Story: Mimi explained that arguing about teeth takes 20% of their Brain
Battery, but if they just automate it like a robot, it only takes 1%. This saves
energy for gaming!
 THE GLITCH ('I'll do it tomorrow')
The Science: This is 'Temporal Discounting'. Your brain cares more about
being lazy NOW than having healthy teeth in the FUTURE.
Correction: You have to remind your Brain Wizard: 'Future Me will be very
angry if Present Me leaves him with a giant hole in his tooth!'
 THE TOOL ('The 2-Minute DJ')
The Science: When you are bored, 2 minutes feels like an hour. This is called
'Time Dilation'. The fix? Dopamine distraction! 
In The Story: Dilo played epic Boss Fight music. When the song starts, you
brush. When the song stops, you stop. Your brain focuses on the heavy beat,
not the boring scrubbing.
