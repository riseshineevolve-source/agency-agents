WELCOME TO PROJECT UNSTOPPABLE

31 DAYS.
SIX HAPPY MAKERS.
ONE LIFE THAT ACTUALLY HAS TO WORK.

This is not a school book, a personality makeover, or a
31-day promise to become a flawless human.

It is a field guide for real life:
hard starts, awkward moments, friendship drama, big plans,
bad results, money choices, pressure, distraction, and the
days when your brain has apparently opened seventeen tabs.

The Happy Makers are bringing the tools.
You are doing the field tests.

Every day you will:
TRY something.
BUILD something useful.
TEST what happens.
KEEP what earns a place in your kit.

No perfect streak required.
Miss a day? Come back.
Hate a tool? Drop it.
Find one that works? Keep it.

YOU ARE THE CAPTAIN.

By Day 31, you will not have a new personality.
You will have better moves.

READY? TAKE THE SEVENTH SEAT.

DAY 6: BRIEFING
MAIN QUEST: The Two-Sided Interview

Pick a parent, caregiver, teacher, coach, or other trusted
adult you feel safe talking to.

Choose ONE or TWO:

"What rule did you think was unfair when you were my age?"

"What did adults misunderstand about you back then?"

"When you worry about me, what are you usually trying to
protect or prevent?"

They can skip a question.
So can you.

NOW TEST THE MOVE:

"I hear that ______ matters to you.
Can I tell you what I am planning to do?"

Your job is not to surrender.
Your job is to get both sides out of guessing mode.
