# Gentle Steps EN V2 — Title / Subtitle Owner Decision

Status: OWNER GATE

Polish R4 title directions:
1. **ŚWIĘTA SĄ TEŻ PO DRODZE**
2. **ZANIM ZNOWU BĘDZIE PO ŚWIĘTACH**

The goal is transcreation, not literal translation.

## A — RECOMMENDED: BEFORE CHRISTMAS SLIPS BY

### Subtitle A1 — recommended
**24 Ten-Minute Family Moments for Less Rush, More Laughter, and Real Time Together**

Why it works:
- captures the emotional mechanism of `ZANIM ZNOWU BĘDZIE PO ŚWIĘTACH`;
- natural native English;
- creates urgency without sounding stressful;
- clearly speaks to the parent buying the book;
- subtitle explains the product immediately.

### Subtitle A2
**A 24-Day Family Countdown of Games, Conversations, and Time Together**

More descriptive / less emotional.

### Subtitle A3
**24 Days of Games, Laughter, and Small Moments Worth Keeping**

Warmer and more giftable, less explicit about ten minutes.

---

## B — MOST FAITHFUL TO POLISH IDEA: CHRISTMAS HAPPENS ALONG THE WAY

### Subtitle B1 — recommended
**24 Ten-Minute Family Moments to Slow the Rush, Laugh More, and Be Together**

Why it works:
- best English transcreation of `ŚWIĘTA SĄ TEŻ PO DRODZE`;
- expresses the book's deepest idea: Christmas is not only the final perfect day;
- warm, premium, distinctive.

Risk:
- main title is more literary and needs the subtitle to explain the product.

### Subtitle B2
**A 24-Day Family Countdown of Games, Conversations, and Everyday Christmas Moments**

---

## C — STRONGEST MARKETING HOOK: DON'T MISS CHRISTMAS ON THE WAY TO CHRISTMAS

### Subtitle C1
**24 Ten-Minute Family Moments for Less Rush, More Laughter, and More Time Together**

Why it works:
- says the product problem instantly;
- memorable;
- very aligned with the final Happy Makers message.

Risk:
- longer and more slogan-like;
- less elegant than A/B.

---

## D — CLEAN / MODERN: CHRISTMAS, IN BETWEEN EVERYTHING

### Subtitle D1
**24 Ten-Minute Games, Pauses, and Conversations for Busy Families**

Why it works:
- captures real December life;
- immediately fits the US/UK family reality.

Risk:
- less emotional / less timeless;
- `in between` can read slightly lifestyle/self-help.

---

## E — LEGACY BRAND OPTION: 24 GENTLE STEPS TO CHRISTMAS

### Subtitle E1
**Less Rush. More Laughter. Ten Minutes Together Each Day.**

Why keep:
- preserves legacy recognition.

Why it ranks lower:
- `Gentle Steps` undersells how funny, active and modern the rewritten book now is;
- can still suggest mindfulness/self-help, which the new edition deliberately moved away from.

## Best if matching the Polish emotional concept more directly

**24 Ten-Minute Family Moments to Slow the Rush, Laugh More, and Be Together**

## Packaging note

Do not use a literal English translation:
- `Christmas Is Also Along the Way` = unnatural;
- `Before It Is Christmas No More Again` = impossible/unnatural;
- `Before Christmas Is Over Again` = understandable but less elegant and more melancholy.

The English title should reproduce the effect of the Polish title, not its syntax.
