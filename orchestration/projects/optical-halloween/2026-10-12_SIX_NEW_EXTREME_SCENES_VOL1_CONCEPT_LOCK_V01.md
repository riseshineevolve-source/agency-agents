## N1 — THE PORTER UNDER THE HOUSE (Winda bez dna)
Distinct room: vertical servant/dumbwaiter lift shaft. Unused hero: imposing living porter assembled from old suitcases and wide striped straps, neither human ghost nor existing animal. Camera: exact 90° straight UP from shaft floor, extreme low-angle. Optical event: elevator door frames and suitcase strap lines align into an anamorphic endless tower; huge near foreground lock versus receding roof. NO mirror or reflected skeleton. Narrative: a suitcase packed for the guest hangs between arrival and exit.

## N2 — THE OCTOPUS ABOVE THE ABYSS (Cysterna w suficie)
Room: subterranean rainwater cistern, not a mirror floor, no ballroom/cathedral. New hero: one majestic patterned octopus, eight physically plausible broad white colorable arms. Camera: EXACT 90° TOP-DOWN into the cistern. Optical illusion: interleaved architectural cistern rings and arms alternately read as convex terraces or an endless well; one arm reaches across two incompatible depth planes (carefully visualized). NO reflection. Narrative: retrieves the missing minute from the submerged heart of the house.

## N3 — THE GLASSBLOWER'S WRONG HORIZON (Pracownia soczewek)
Room: lensmaker's glassworks with large furnace and 3 overscale curved glass panels; not a mirror gallery. New hero: glassblower with huge protective apron and one giant transparent spherical lens at end of tube. Whole-camera roll +110 degrees CLOCKWISE; architecture, furnace, hanging tools all rolled, not just staircase. Optical event: real refraction/anamorphic distortion changes the contour of hero and room only inside lens, turning a stretched white shape into a recognizable visitor. No duplicate character or reflection. Narrative: establishes the true viewing angle for the eventual invitation.

## N4 — THE PHOTOGRAPHER OF MISSING SHADOWS (Ciemnia)
Room: wet photographic darkroom, suspended drying prints, enlarger and chemistry trays. New hero: mysterious photographer with a bellows-camera head, silhouette clear and white colorable surfaces. Whole-world camera roll -110 degrees COUNTERCLOCKWISE. Optical event: strictly bounded positive/negative inverse images (black-white reversal) on photographic paper reveal the missing person but not a skeleton; architectural lines stay crisp and 1-bit. No mirrors/polished floors. Narrative: visual evidence that the house held an arrival, not a prisoner.

## N5 — THE WAX HOST SERVES THE CEILING (Jadalnia slużby)
Distinct room: intimate servants' dining room, enormous narrow table viewed in strong lateral profile. New protagonist: elegant living wax steward, broad white streams of wax forming its anatomical body and one hand offering a plate. Optical event: dual gravity separated across a folded/table plane: candles drip DOWN on one side and UP on the other, while the steward bridges both; 3D broad-line wax currents make a coherent flip. No floor reflection or generic ghost. Narrative: the final place setting has been waiting for its invited guest.

## N6 — THE WALLPAPER ESCAPE (Pracownia sklejania tapet)
Room: restoration/paper-folding atelier, wall seen in extreme SIDE CUTAWAY not a whole-house dollhouse view. Unused hero: the Paper Walker, a distinctive person emerging from large folded blank wallpaper shapes; a human silhouette is readable only via negative-space intersections. Optical event: 2D/3D ambiguity, a strip of black/white paper folded through 3 spatial planes produces apparent doorway only from the viewer's forced-perspective eye level. NO mirror, no skeleton; not an ordinary H-16 invitation icon. Narrative: a white route from within walls to the last threshold is finally physically visible.
