### // FILE: BRAIN_HACK — TOP SECRET SCIENCE (For Big and Small Brains)

Why do video games make you grumpy when you turn them off? Video games are designed by neuroscientists to trigger constant dopamine hits. Your brain loves this, but to protect itself from overflowing, it reduces the number of dopamine receptors (Downregulation). When the game turns off, your brain suddenly has very low dopamine levels, making you feel instantly irritable, angry, and depressed. Getting outside in the sun helps reset those levels!
