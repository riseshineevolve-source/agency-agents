# Terminology and Recurring Labels

## Keep / protected
- Happy Makers
- MAIN QUEST
- XP BOOST
- TOOL FOR LIFE (RESCUE KIT)
- SYSTEM REBOOT
- GLITCH & PUZZLE
- FREE ROAM
- First-Aid Kit / Rescue Kit concept
- Dilo / Luli

## Under review
- MINDFULNESS: redundant with SYSTEM REBOOT and often pushes the register toward wellness/coaching.
- Avatar, server, algorithm, hack, glitch: retain when they sharpen meaning; reduce when they become wallpaper.
- superpower: keep selectively; avoid using it to deny real difficulty.

## Forbidden claim style
- rewires your brain / confidence chemicals / universal biological hacks;
- X is always fear or they do this because...;
- class-coded rich/poor moralizing;
- diagnosis/treatment language.
