# An Activity Advent Calendar That Doesn't Give Parents More Work

An activity Advent calendar sounds lovely. Until you're the one coming up with 24 ideas, finding the supplies and filling the envelopes after everyone else has gone to bed.

Chocolate calendars are fun. So are handmade ones. But there's another kind of countdown: **the surprise isn't a new thing to open. It's something you get to do together.**

That's the thinking behind **Before Christmas Slips By**, a family Advent book with 24 ready-made days. No craft prep, no extra shopping list and no need to invent tomorrow's activity tonight.

## What do the days actually look like?

Here's a peek at **three different days**. None of them gives away a complete page.

### LET'S TAKE A MOMENT

The TV is on. Someone's watching a video. Notifications keep chiming, and somebody is trying to tell a story over it all. Don't demand total silence. Just turn **one** sound down for a minute and listen to what's left. A tiny change, right in the room you're already in.

### LET'S DO THIS TOGETHER

**Reverse Charades.** One person guesses while everyone else acts out the same thing at once. Say the prompt is “building a snowman.” Nobody agrees on the actions beforehand. Suddenly three people appear to be competing in entirely different winter sports. That's the fun of it.

### LET'S TALK

**“What little things have we picked up from each other?”**

A phrase your dad always says. A song your child got stuck in everyone's head. The way Grandma makes a sandwich. The funny part is discovering how much of your family you carry around without ever having thought about it.

## Built for ordinary December evenings

Some nights you'll have ten minutes. Some nights you'll have five, or only two people will want to join. A skipped day stays skipped. There's nothing to make up and no perfect streak to protect.

Across the 24 days, **Before Christmas Slips By** moves through three simple parts: **LET'S TAKE A MOMENT / LET'S DO THIS TOGETHER / LET'S TALK**. Games include **TWO PEOPLE** versions, and each day has a small **TRY IT TOMORROW** idea. The activities don't call for extra purchases or elaborate setup.

It's not about making every night unforgettable. It's about having something ready when someone says, **“Can we do something together?”**
