## Rules for every visual
Single continuous printed-coloring illustration, not comic panels/contact board. Hero 35-65% composition except landscape architecture wide. Dominant physically reasoned optical event, at most 1 secondary discovery. Bounded reflective apparatus; test subject vs reflection correspondence except a clear *single* magical contradiction. Camera tilt must rotate entire world rather than merely stairs. Save independent v01/v02 with unchanged previous best. Reject "cathedral + repeat spiral + reflected skeleton" formula. At 1m thumbnail immediate perceptual WTF; at printed 8.5x11 form/colorability. No fake QA pass.
