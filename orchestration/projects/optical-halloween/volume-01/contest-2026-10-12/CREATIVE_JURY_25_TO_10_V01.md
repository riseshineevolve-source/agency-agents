## Non-negotiable visual QA
* Each final candidate a single full portrait 8.5x11-inch art piece, not a collage of five.
* 100% #000000 and #FFFFFF, no grayscale or gradient (including inside black masses), no hatching, no micro-lines, strong ink outlines ~1.5-3 mm at print size, broad CLOSED white coloring islands >12 mm narrowest width. Optical form must be 65-80% of idea, not decorative wallpaper.
* No Gothic halls or repeated cathedrals, moons, stars, mirrored floors, generic spiral tunnels, ornate doors or staircase grids, skulls/ghosts/cats/bats/ravens/owl/fox/spider/snake/violinists/marionettes. No wording ANYWHERE.
* At thumbnail, two variants must look radically different. Camera roll by 110° = rotate entire architecture/gravity relationships, not just character/props.
* Brand, optical physics, and print finish have veto, no GOLD until art evaluated visually. Save failures and finalists without overwriting accepted 14 source images.
