# Optical Halloween Illustration Studio

Status: ACTIVE / ILLUSTRATIONS ONLY
Date: 2026-10-08

## Scope lock
Create premium Optical Halloween hero illustrations only.
Do not create seek-and-find pages, masks, exact tokens, answer maps, pagination, book assembly, KDP files, or factory integration.
Optical Animals is read-only reference material and must not be mutated.

## Inherited visual DNA
- premium gallery-art black/white identity;
- strong hero subject and thumbnail read;
- approximately 40–55% solid black where composition benefits;
- broad clean white coloring zones;
- patterns must follow anatomy/perspective and interact with the scene;
- accurate anatomy and coherent reflection/perspective logic;
- no fuzzy fur, chibi, sticker, mandala, zentangle, clip-art, generic stock Halloween;
- unusual editorial viewpoints and strong silhouette;
- collection coherence comes from finish and optical intelligence, not repeated background templates.

## First concept round — 2026-10-08
1. BLACK CAT / IMPOSSIBLE STAIRCASE
   A sleek black cat crossing a gravity-defying stairwell seen from an extreme low angle. Stairs fold into walls and ceiling; cat shadow creates a secondary bat/portal shape. Large white stair planes remain colorable.
2. RAVEN WING PORTAL
   Raven in three-quarter flight; outer flight feathers become a deep architectural tunnel while the bird remains anatomically clear. Portal is built from feather rhythm, not pasted behind it.
3. HAUNTED LIBRARY FOLD
   Wide interior where shelves bend through impossible corners into a paper-like spiral. One ghost exists only as missing negative-space books and gaps, never as a cute outlined figure.
4. MASKED MIRROR BALL
   Elegant Halloween masquerade viewed through fractured mirrors and kaleidoscopic floor geometry. Human figures are secondary; masks and reflections create the optical mechanism.
5. PUMPKIN MAZE FACE
   Monumental pumpkin seen in steep foreshortening. Its carved face is not drawn directly; the eyes and mouth emerge from a continuous labyrinth cut into the surface and surrounding architecture.

## Generation intent for OH-001
8.5x11 portrait, premium black-and-white optical illustration.
One adult sleek black cat, anatomically natural and non-cute, walking across an impossible staircase system.
Extreme low-angle editorial camera, asymmetrical diagonal composition.
Stairs fold into vertical walls and ceiling while preserving one internally coherent optical rule.
Cat silhouette remains instantly readable and dominant.
Large white stair faces and architectural planes remain usable for coloring.
Optical bands and checker distortions bend with perspective, not as flat wallpaper.
A secondary hidden visual layer should emerge from the cat's shadow/negative space without adding a second literal animal.
No pumpkins, no graveyard, no witch broom, no kawaii ghost, no mandala, no text, no frame.
Keep linework crisp, avoid gray airbrush and micro-noise.
