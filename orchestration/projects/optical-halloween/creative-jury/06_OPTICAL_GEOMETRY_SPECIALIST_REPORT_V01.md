### Construction rules used in the routes

- **Plane mirrors:** locate the virtual point by reflecting the real point across the mirror plane. The image has the object's actual scale; apparent scale then depends on camera projection. For plane `n·X=d` with unit normal `n`, use `X'=X−2(n·X−d)n`. A camera-to-virtual-point ray must intersect the finite mirror inside its boundary and have an unobstructed real reflected path. This implements the equal-distance virtual-image construction described by [OpenStax, Images Formed by Plane Mirrors](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/2-1-images-formed-by-plane-mirrors).
- **Floor:** with the reflective plane at `z=0`, a source point `(x,y,z)` maps to `(x,y,−z)`. Foot contacts stay on the plane. No lateral hand substitution, second action, or time reversal occurs. The ray/finite-surface rule above also applies.
- **Shadows:** from source `L` through each actual exposed boundary point `P`, intersect `L+t(P−L)` with the receiving wall. White drawing areas on cloth still denote opaque material. Hidden ribs cannot produce separate transmitted shadows.
- **Refraction:** trace the air/glass/water boundaries with `n1 sin θ1 = n2 sin θ2`; do not flip a drawing arbitrarily. This boundary law is supported by [OpenStax, Refraction](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/1-3-refraction). A cylinder has optical power principally across its curved cross-section, rather than spherical power in both axes; see [Edmund Optics, What are Cylinder Lenses?](https://www.edmundoptics.com/knowledge-center/application-notes/optics/what-are-cylinder-lenses/). Applying that principle to a water jug is an inference requiring its actual wall, liquid, object, and eye geometry. It is not a claim that every water jug produces the proposed image.
- **Anamorphosis and forced perspective:** construct the desired image from the selected eye point before assigning its parts to real planes. For camera origin `C`, points on the same projected ray have form `C+t·v`. Their supporting planes and occlusion order must remain ordinary. A sideways view or removal of one real occluder must defeat the apparent obstruction.
- **Hinge seam:** permit one explicitly located adjacency mismatch. Keep instrument loads, cup, stop, keyboard, and all geometry away from that seam ordinary. No second return loop, scaled person, or duplicated room may hide inside the design.

These are construction prescriptions, not executed ray traces or engineering certification.
