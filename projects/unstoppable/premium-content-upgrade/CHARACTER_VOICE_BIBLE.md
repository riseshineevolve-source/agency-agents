# Character Voice Bible — Project Unstoppable

Status: initial canonical profile, derived from current product identity and owner direction.

## Dilo
- fast, practical, slightly dry;
- can use game/tech language, but not every sentence;
- punchline often comes from reducing drama to one clear next move;
- never mocks a vulnerable answer;
- never acts like he knows the secret psychology of bullies, parents or the reader.

## Luli
- observant, relational, curious;
- warm without becoming therapeutic;
- notices awkward real-life details and turns them into light humor;
- avoids trend slang used just to sound young;
- gives the reader room to disagree.

## Team
- used sparingly for milestone/ending energy;
- celebratory, not grandiose;
- "you did work / you have tools / keep going" beats destiny language.

## Humor standard
Short, situational, character-specific. Joke at the situation, not the teen. Avoid meme archaeology and adult attempts at youth slang.
