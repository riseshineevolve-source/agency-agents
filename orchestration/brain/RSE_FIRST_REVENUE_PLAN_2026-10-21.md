## Definition of a complete release packet

Existing-asset lookup: Library metadata resolves the older
`24 Gentle Steps to Christmas COVER HARDCOVER.pdf` and copies, plus
`The Happy-Makers Present 24 Gentle Steps to Christmas final.pdf`.
These are recovery candidates, not verified current-title/format masters;
contents and dimensions were not inspected in this management pass. A hardcover
cover must not be silently reused as paperback. The bounded search did not
establish a current OA full-wrap PDF; absence is not proven. OA writer must
identify the actual current cover rather than generate a replacement by default.

One folder/manifest must identify:

1. Actual interior PDF, page count, print dimensions and SHA256.
2. Actual full-wrap cover PDF matched to current trim/paper/ink/page count and SHA256.
3. Approved title/subtitle/author/language, truthful description, keywords/categories.
4. Exact ISBN choice and any account/payment/tax-setup blocker, without exposing credentials.
5. Focused preflight, Print Previewer result and representative physical proof
   wherever the existing product gate requires it.
6. Source/rights provenance and truthful KDP AI-generated-content declaration.
7. One recommended launch price with current actual print cost/royalty evidence.
8. One consolidated owner decision on those exact files, price and submission.

No request for approval of a vague future result. Missing files are named with an
owner/next action; a status report or a contact sheet is not the release packet.

KDP requires both interior and cover files. Its guidance distinguishes Draft,
In Review and Live; review timing varies by product type. AI-generated artwork
requires the applicable KDP disclosure. Do not promise sale availability today
or use a false low-content classification to simplify submission.

## Stop the work that delays selling

- No new apps, book concepts, series naming exercises, generic factories or
  broad refactors before the first launch packet is complete.
- No more alternate artwork unless a current owner request or a proven release
  blocker requires it. Preserve approved originals and the best existing versions.
- No repeated full audits/renders after local corrections; review affected pages
  plus their dependency closure, keeping mandatory proof/quality checks.
- Full Website redesign, PL site completion,48-prompt expansion, AI indexing,
  Google Play approval and other products are not dependencies for KDP release.
- Stop retrying GitHub Actions while its confirmed account budget blocks runner
  startup; keep verified local receipts. No paid/billing change is authorized.
- Finish a current atomic change and checkpoint safely before parking a lane;
  do not discard another chat's ongoing work or create a replacement writer.
