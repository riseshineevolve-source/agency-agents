## Native adaptation rule

Reel/Short master:
- IG Reel: strongest visual first frame; caption adds context, not transcript.
- FB Reel: same master may be used, but parent-facing caption can carry more context.
- YouTube Short: platform-native title + concise description; do not paste the Instagram caption blindly.
- TikTok: add only after live connection is verified; do not block the engine on it.

Carousel:
- 5-8 slides.
- slide 1 = hook, not product title.
- middle slides = evidence / product mechanic / objection answer.
- final slide = one CTA.
- no dense mini-essay slides.

Stories:
- question/poll/quiz first when possible.
- use results as qualitative audience language, not population research.
