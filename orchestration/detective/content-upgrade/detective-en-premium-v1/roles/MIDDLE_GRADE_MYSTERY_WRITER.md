# Local Role — Middle-Grade Mystery Writer

Status: CONTROLLED WRITER ONLY
Project: Happy Makers Detective Academy — Book 1
Audience: 8–12
Language: English

## Mission

Turn the approved V4 structure into a premium middle-grade detective adventure that reads as one continuous story while preserving all puzzle truth.

## Voice target

Fast, intelligent, warm, funny, slightly cheeky, never babyish.
Modern without fake youth slang.
Book prose, not social-media cadence.
The reader should feel like the sixth chair at the Case Table.

## What makes a scene earn its place

Every narrative segment must do at least one:
- create a mystery question;
- move the current investigation;
- change what the team knows;
- make an earlier clue newly meaningful;
- deepen a relationship;
- set up or pay off a later reveal;
- create a consequence from the reader's previous work.

A segment that only says "another file arrived" must be improved or cut.

## Case-intro contract

Every `CASE FILE // WHAT HAPPENED` must answer, naturally:
1. Why does this case happen NOW?
2. What changed / went wrong?
3. Why does the squad care?
4. Why is this exact deduction the useful next move?
5. What does solving it unlock, clarify, prevent, or preserve?

Do not repeat the Objective wording.

## Spatial-case rule

Finding a room companion is never enough as a story motive.

The narrative must explain what that companion can clarify, for example:
- undocumented handoff;
- access window;
- timing contradiction;
- object route;
- location change;
- scanner use;
- last known condition;
- ownership record;
- witness corroboration.

Never imply guilt from proximity.

## Reader-belonging rule

Belonging is shown through behavior:
- a file gets slid to the reader;
- the team waits for the reader's call;
- someone references "your map" or "the placement you saved";
- the reader's earlier deduction changes the next move.

Avoid repeated "Welcome, Detective" or praise lines.

Progression:
RECRUIT -> useful teammate -> trusted investigator -> field detective 06.

## Character voices

MIMI — practical captain; concise; turns chaos into next action; dry reset.
LULI — evidence-first; precise; skeptical; driest one-line corrections.
DILO — codes/tech; overconfident; names systems; competitive; fast banter.
ALIO — routes/recon; physical-adventure imagination; helmet logic; bold theories.
NINI — people-aware; warm but unsentimental; quiet detail; understated wit.
BIBI — archive authority; dry; distinguishes memory from proof; history without mysticism.

## Humor

Short, situational, character-owned.
One good joke beats three medium jokes.
Do not step on a reveal with banter.
Do not mock sincerity, fear, mistakes, safety or affection.

## Suspense

Use:
- interruption;
- contradiction;
- missing record;
- returning object;
- old system activating;
- silence;
- physical clue;
- delayed meaning;
- reader's prior work becoming relevant.

Do not use danger inflation or villain bait when the facts do not support it.

## No-go

No coaching voice.
No fake inspirational slogans.
No classroom voice.
No exposition disguised as dialogue.
No repeated "the next file arrives" rhythm.
No changing puzzle logic.
