# Independent First-Pass Diagnostic Board

This is diagnostic only. Review lanes were kept independent in judgment before editorial synthesis.

## 1. Teen psychology / safety / consent reviewer
**KEEP:** naming emotions, asking for help, boundaries, rest, breaking tasks down.
**FIX:** unsupported mechanism claims; mind-reading bullies/parents; required disclosure/photo/social tasks; money/class framing.
**BLOCK:** stale First-Aid callbacks until safety wording is synchronized.

## 2. Teen audience / anti-cringe reviewer
**KEEP:** mission language, Free Roam, short challenges, practical tools.
**FIX:** dated meme slang and adults-performing-youth phrasing; too many gamer metaphors; "hell yes" is unnecessary for a 10–14 global book.
**Reason:** the book should feel current because situations are real, not because it chases slang.

## 3. Happy Makers humor / character voice reviewer
**KEEP:** dry lines such as "REST IS NOT A REWARD. IT'S A HARDWARE REQUIREMENT." and "A PLAN ISN'T A PRISON..."
**FIX:** Dilo/Luli are insufficiently differentiated; many jokes are generic tech metaphors instead of character humor.
**Rule:** supporting friends, never mini-coaches.

## 4. Logic / continuity editor
**KEEP:** 31-day sequence and six-part rhythm.
**FIX:** back-matter callbacks must be generated from final day truth; Day 10 cross-surface difference must stay intentional.
**BLOCK:** puzzle slots that promise a puzzle without sufficient problem data.

## 5. Puzzle / activity completeness reviewer
**BLOCK:** majority of legacy print puzzle pages are placeholders, missing diagrams/clues/grids, or show the answer immediately.
**Reason:** a premium workbook cannot ask a child to "solve the puzzle" when the puzzle is absent.

## 6. Native English / book register editor
**KEEP:** mostly clear short sentences and strong scanability.
**FIX:** inconsistent slogan register, dated slang, pseudo-science, unnecessary capitals, and "MINDFULNESS" label drift.
**Do not:** turn the book into formal educational prose.

## 7. Structure / function-distribution reviewer
**KEEP:** predictable daily skeleton.
**FIX:** repetitive visualization resets, repeated drawing prompts, social asks, and late-book reflection cluster.
**Direction:** vary mechanics, not brand architecture.

## 8. Meaning Guardian / source convergence
**LOCK:** reviewed revival-branch safer copy outranks the older PDF on the documented sync set.
**LOCK:** app-only Day 10 game does not overwrite the print Tangram mechanic without a separate decision.
