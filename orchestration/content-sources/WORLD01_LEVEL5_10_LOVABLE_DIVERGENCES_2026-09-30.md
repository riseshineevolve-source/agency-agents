## Source-derived structures absent or flattened in the legacy model

without inventing mechanics:

  preserved as an `exercise` node with `visual_only_fields` for diagram text
  not available in PDF text extraction.

- Level 10: printed `// FILE: SYSTEM_TIPS` / **SYSTEM TIP** block.
- Printed punctuation, speaker boundaries, system-log ordering and labels remain
  source-controlled.

## Reuse disposition

**Reuse from Lovable where it survives product QA:** visual language, character
assets subject to asset review, mobile card/navigation ideas, story-step
progression UI, read-aloud/accessibility concepts, map concepts, animation and
sound patterns.

**Do not reuse as canonical truth:** `storyContent.ts` copy, mission keys,
objectives, invented XP values, completion rewards, paywall assumptions,
Lemon Squeezy flow, Supabase-required entry, or any World 01 behavior that
cannot be traced to the published source or the approved App Factory runtime
contract.
