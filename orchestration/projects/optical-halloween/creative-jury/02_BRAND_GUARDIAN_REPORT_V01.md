## 2. Premium identity and anti-kitsch rubric

### HOUSE identity: hospitality under architectural tension

The house is an interrupted act of receiving someone. Its visual signature should be heavy curved domestic structure pressing around unusually generous white receiving spaces: a door return, drawer bay, canopy panel, tile face, cup bowl, raised fallboard, bed plane, stair opening, or board square. Each scene changes the meaning of an ordinary action through one accountable optical cause.

Halloween comes from midnight, stalled preparation, a silent invitation, a distorted first interpretation, and the missing completion of a movement. Fashionable restraint comes from cropped scale, asymmetrical composition, clear silhouettes, and precise material boundaries. Neither a familiar spooky prop nor a decorative brand motif can supply the identity.

**Material grammar:** black lacquer door and piano masses; a horizontal polished foyer floor; white plaster and receiving recesses; opaque umbrella fabric; overlapping slate; cold cast iron; clear water in one cylinder; carved headboard wood; ordinary stair construction; a sparse household board. Material identity is carried by contour, join, support, and band direction, without gray texture.

**Band grammar:** a few thick, unequal, sculptural curves on each dominant structure. Their direction follows an edge, fold, hollow, load-bearing member, overlap, or tension path. At the largest scale, the viewer should read bent or inverted physical space before naming the everyday prop. Approximately 70% optical-world dominance is a compositional brief, not a measured percentage or a demand for 70% patterned pixels.
