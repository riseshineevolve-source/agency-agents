# Function / Repetition Map — Initial Audit

## What works
The six-part daily rhythm is a recognizable brand structure. It creates predictability and a sense of progress. Do not dismantle it merely because it repeats.

## Repeated functions that need rebalancing
1. **System Reboot**: too many visualization/close-eyes exercises. Several are the same emotional job in different metaphors.
2. **Workbench**: repeated "draw a thing / make a map / write a list" without enough mechanic variation.
3. **XP Boost**: frequent requirement to text/tell/interview a family member or friend; this can feel socially demanding and assumes access.
4. **Intro voice**: repeated gamer/server/algorithm framing makes Dilo and Luli sound more alike than intended.
5. **Late book**: Days 28–31 stack identity/legacy/gratitude/review/contract functions and risk reflection fatigue.
6. **Puzzle slot**: often functions as a placeholder rather than a playable payoff. This is a substantive product defect, not just visual production debt.

## Arc implication
Keep the daily skeleton. Upgrade novelty inside each slot: more choice, mini-experiments, observation, ranking, scenario decisions, tiny real-world actions, and actually solvable puzzles.
