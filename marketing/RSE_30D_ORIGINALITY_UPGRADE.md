# RSE 30-Day Marketing Upgrade — Originality Layer

Status: ACTIVE UPGRADE
Date: 2026-09-28
Owner: RSE Marketing Autopilot
Purpose: upgrade the existing Q4 marketing engine without replacing it.

## Rule
Do not restart the existing 30-day plan. Keep canonical product priorities, signature series, platform roles, owner gates, and performance loop. Upgrade the creative packaging so the content feels surprising, useful, brand-specific, and difficult to copy.

## Creative doctrine
RSE must not become another account posting generic parenting tips, motivational quotes, or product slides.

Every master should aim to create at least one of:
- a pattern interrupt,
- an unexpected visual mechanic,
- a repeatable branded format,
- a useful micro-tool,
- a surprising product proof,
- a memorable point of view.

Core brand truth remains:
**Useful in the moment, not just correct in theory.**

## Hook rules
Avoid:
- "5 tips..."
- "Did you know?"
- "Boost your child's..."
- "Unlock their potential"
- broad inspirational claims.

Prefer:
- concrete messy moment,
- surprising sentence,
- category break,
- object/mechanic,
- identity invitation,
- unresolved curiosity.
