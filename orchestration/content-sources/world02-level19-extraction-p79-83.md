## p80

1. SYSTEM LOG >> Sunday Morning. Plan: Water Park. Alio was fully geared up: Swimsuit, flippers, goggles, and a snorkel. He looked like a highly trained frog-man ready for a water mission in the hallway. Then... THUNDER. Rain smashed against the window like angry rocks.
2. MIMI — Crew... I'm sorry. Mission Abort. The Water Park is closed due to lightning.
3. ALIO — NO! YOU PROMISED! YOU ARE A LIAR! YOU CONTROL THE WEATHER!
4. SYSTEM LOG >> Alio threw his goggles across the room. He flopped onto the floor, flapping his flippers against the wood like a stuck, very angry seal. Nini started crying quietly, holding her pink pool noodle.
5. NINI — My heart bucket is broken! I wanted the big slide! The water park was my dream!

## p81

1. DILO — Great. Now my weekend is ruined. I hate rain. I'm going back to bed to be lazy. I will become one with the mattress and never play soccer again.
2. MIMI — Stop. We can be sad. It hurts. But calling me a liar won't stop the rain. (Mimi says their feelings are okay, but immediately jumps up and down, shaking her arms to literally shake the stress out of her body, showing them how to reset). We hit a wall. We have two choices. Option A: We cry for 4 hours, ruin the day, and eat dry toast. Option B: We PIVOT.
3. ALIO — Pivot?
4. MIMI — First, we do the Shake-Out Dance! Everyone, stand up! Shake your hands! Shake your legs! Wiggle the mad energy right out of your muscles! (Mimi jumps up and down, shaking her arms wildly).

## p82

1. LULI — (Shaking one hand very logically) We change direction. Like in basketball. Plan: 'Indoor Beach'. I am taking charge of the planning. I have already figured out the exact heat needed to make it feel like Hawaii. I've turned the heater up and placed a fan behind the toaster to make a warm, bread-smelling ocean breeze. Alio, I need you to design the shape of the towel-beach so no one kicks each other.
2. DILO — That sounds... silly.
3. ALIO — (Suddenly interested, wiping his tears) Can we play video games on the beach? And can I build a sandcastle out of sofa cushions?
4. MIMI — Yes. Beach rules apply. Alio, stand up. Stop flapping. You are not a seal. Pivot with me. Keep one foot down and turn the other way.
