# Finale Concept Competition — Project Unstoppable V2

Date: 2026-10-05
Baseline final mechanic: the locked Day 31 meta-puzzle resolves to RESET.

## Concept A — The Seventh Seat
Payoff: reveal that the reader has gradually become the seventh seat at the Happy Makers table.
Mechanic: Day 31 builds a personal field kit; page 189 is a short six-voice welcome.
Strength: strongest emotional attachment to the ensemble.
Risk: could become sentimental if overwritten.

## Concept B — Field Kit Graduation
Payoff: reader chooses five tools and creates a rapid-access card.
Mechanic: final days are an evidence-based "graduation."
Strength: strongest practical transfer.
Risk: "graduation" can make the team relationship feel finished, when owner wants desire for Part 2.

## Concept C — Case File: You
Payoff: reader solves a final evidence case using their own pages.
Mechanic: Day 30–31 becomes a detective meta-case.
Strength: strongest cross-brand Detective Academy flavor.
Risk: changes Project Unstoppable's product identity too far toward a detective book.

## Concept D — Open Mission
Payoff: the Happy Makers hand the reader an unscripted real-life mission for the next seven days.
Mechanic: reader designs the mission and reports evidence.
Strength: strong autonomy.
Risk: weaker emotional closure and less satisfying use of RESET.

## Editor-in-Chief decision
Concept A wins clearly, but borrows the strongest practical mechanism from B.

Final direction:
**THE SEVENTH SEAT + PERSONAL FIELD KIT**

- D28 = choose character words and behaviors.
- D29 = notice good without erasing hard.
- D30 = evidence audit across the whole book.
- D31 = choose five tools, write a reset route, unlock RESET.
- Page 189 = the six Happy Makers explicitly welcome the reader to the seventh seat.
- Ending stays open: the crew relationship continues; this is not a farewell/graduation.

No detective-case overlay is imported into the book.
