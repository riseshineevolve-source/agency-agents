## Experience verdict

- Every day leaves a concrete artifact, decision, test result, or reusable tool.
- Difficulty rises by independence and tool-combination, not by making every day mechanically longer.
- Checkpoints on Days 7, 14, 21 and 27 require the reader to reuse earlier tools.
- Days 30–31 require evidence selection and integration across the whole book.
- Social/external tasks have private, delayed, or preparation fallbacks where availability can block the session.
- Safety, refusal, disclosure and support choices remain opt-in and age-appropriate.
