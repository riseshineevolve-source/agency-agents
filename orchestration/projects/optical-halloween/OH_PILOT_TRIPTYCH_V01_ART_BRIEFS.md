### H-01 — The Key Without Teeth / THE HOUSE THAT REMEMBERS
**Scene position/role:** establish missing minute and reversible domestic world. A visitor's real hand holds a short TOOTHLESS key before a living townhouse doorway. In an actual angled, planar vertical mirror built into the door-jamb, three black rail divisions behind the visitor align precisely with the reflected key's blank edge and *seem* to complete three teeth. The key itself has not changed. One subtle white invitation-seat negative-space emerges between large hinge blades. Next scene: the floor/shadow of a person appears to arrive early.

**Visual Storyteller choice:** Competition H-01 candidate A, pending real image quality. Refuse gigantic gothic lock, floating key or horror hallway.

**Render camera:** extreme intimate three-quarter oblique, key+natural adult hand in strongly foreshortened foreground lower left, slender real mirror narrow vertical right of focal key, folded townhouse threshold receding diagonally up and away. No literal cat in H-01.

**Optical rule:** mirror plane reflects actual key and the three background iron rails. Their carefully aligned silhouettes produce the apparent teeth in the glass alone. Viewer should understand the real key has no teeth. A secondary negative-space empty chair motif is formed by one bright doorway gap, not by drawn chair.

**Black/white art structure:** big solid black hinge slabs and iron rails, deep black door slot, white palm/knuckle zones and large white interior planes behind mirror, a few medium-weight stable parallel contours following architecture. Density calm around central key and hand.

**Reality Checker pre-gate:** no extra fingers, clear mirror edge and real background rails, no fake teeth on physical key, reflection reverses orientation correctly, recognizable one key and one hand. Flag rather than certify if image generation cannot resolve these.

**Prompt for candidate `OH_H01_KEY_WITHOUT_TEETH_v01`:**
A museum-quality, adult/older-kid black-and-white Op Art ink illustration in vertical 8.5:11 composition, extreme cinematic oblique macro perspective of one anatomically plausible adult hand holding a completely toothless antique key in front of a sentient townhouse door, monumental architectural black hinge blades, one slim tilted planar mirror in the door jamb where the key's reflection aligns with THREE actual iron rail bars in the room behind to make the *apparent* teeth only within the mirror; the physical key stays smooth. A secondary welcoming empty-chair silhouette appears subtly between broad white doorway voids, elegantly folded domestic planes lead into depth but only one clearly governed impossible joint; powerful deep black ink masses, broad clean bright white shapes that can actually be colored, crisp deliberate perspective-following contour lines, one near-miraculous optical illusion with clean first-read, no decorative wallpaper, no text, no border, no letters, no realistic shadows rendered as gray gradients, no floating keys, no pumpkin, no ghost, no tiny line noise.

### C-01 — The Fare the Lamp Remembers / THE MIDNIGHT CARNIVAL BELOW THE CITY
**Scene position/role:** deserted underground tram stop; a traveler reaches for a ticket-shaped reflection that is physically projected by a lamp partially occluded by a metal bracket. The owner of the hand begins the story by choosing to touch the light. The missing corner will recur and finally be replaced by participation, not a collectible prize.

**Visual Storyteller choice:** Competition C-01 A. The phenomenon is a rectangular optical *absence*; ticket formed of light is the only intentionally fantastical element, clearly bounded to the plane of enamel glass.

**Render camera:** dramatic knee-height lateral view toward an asymmetrical station kiosk front glass, one adult hand enters from near right, steel gantry supports twist through the quiet station volume. Ignore generic circus iconography except one tiny bat-wing outline embedded in the structural iron joint; far in darkness a hint of stage-fabric can be perceived.

**Optical rule:** real luminous rectangular aperture high behind traveler -> occluding bracket creates clipped corner -> correct geometrically reversed ticket-shaped reflection on black lacquer glass. The ticket remains an optical rectangle on glass, not a floating paper ticket; close enough to touch.

**Black/white art structure:** massive dark enamel/steel anchors, half a dozen broad white rim-lit stair/gantry plates, clean white hand and lamp interior, spare perspective-following stripes show rhythm without generic carnival circles or stars.

**Reality Checker pre-gate:** one hand five fingers, reflection connects to source, no text/ticket letters, no giant translucent second hand, ground contact credible.

**Prompt for candidate `OH_C01_FARE_LAMP_v01`:**
Vertical 8.5:11 black-and-white premium gallery-grade optical ink art of a mysterious EMPTY UNDERGROUND TRAM PLATFORM under a lost midnight carnival; intense low oblique viewpoint from knee height, close-up of an adult hand approaching a polished black enamel ticket kiosk; a real rectangular station lamp and its dark metal bracket, both visible above and behind, reflect into the kiosk's planar glass as one exquisite bright white ticket-like RECTANGLE WITH ONE MISSING CORNER. The 'ticket' appears ONLY as a carefully aligned optical reflection in the black glass, not printed or floating. Monumental diagonal steel canopy planes and an almost imperceptible folded velvet attraction beyond make a deep cinematic composition; sharp controlled ink-black/white blocks and sparse warped perspective geometry, broad large white regions for coloring, a hidden open-palm-shaped negative-space reveal, surreal elegant Halloween atmosphere without typical pumpkins/clowns/cute ghosts/mandalas, no typography, no captions, no fog or gray airbrush, no microscopic striping.

### M-01 — The Gate of Missing Faces / THE ECLIPSE MUSEUM OF LOST NIGHTS
**Scene position/role:** entrance into museum of unlived possibilities. A visitor stands at an arch of porcelain reliefs that have no sculpted faces; three face profiles emerge ONLY AS WHITE NEGATIVE-SPACE cuts between thick interlocking black porcelain planes. The mysterious eclipse is a minuscule unclosed notch in the lintel. A tiny open white doorway is the entry to discover future-bearing glass in M-02/M-04, not an image of the future yet.

**Visual Storyteller choice:** Competition M-01 B. Museum gets a sculptural entrance rather than the House's hinged doors or Carnival's theatrical station.

**Render camera:** ultra-low three-quarter architectural view looking up and across an asymmetrical monumental porcelain relief. Great white profile voids cut through surrounding black sculptural mass, minimal tiny visitor silhouette included only to reveal scale. No big literal face, no literal bird or moon.

**Optical rule:** positive/negative figure-ground switching reveals 3 human face profiles at a different reading distance. The faces are *shapes in gaps* not extra sculpted heads, glass apparitions or haunting portraits.

**Black/white art structure:** crisp porcelain-like white planes with hard black angular joints, giant printable independent white profile interiors, controlled black volume, minimal finely spaced seam engravings constrained to actual form, high sculptural coherence.

**Reality Checker pre-gate:** exactly 3 negative-space faces readable, no ghost heads or literal full masks, consistent profile silhouette, perspective aligns cut-out edges, tiny eclipse motif must not compete with entry.

**Prompt for candidate `OH_M01_GATE_MISSING_FACES_v01`:**
World-class black-and-white gallery Op Art in true ink black and paper white, portrait 8.5:11 extreme worm's-eye asymmetric perspective of the entrance to an impossible nocturnal museum, extraordinary MONUMENTAL ABSTRACT PORCELAIN RELIEF walls folding around a tiny white entry; three elegant human face profiles appear ONLY AS LARGE WHITE NEGATIVE-SPACE GAPS BETWEEN SCULPTURAL BLACK PLANES when viewed from the privileged angle, no actual human heads carved or floating. Hard-edged tessellated porcelain joints, clever visual reversals, accurate converging architecture, just one minute incomplete eclipse notch tucked in the lintel, a tiny real visitor silhouette for scale, imposing yet wistful and safe rather than horror, rich 40–55% dark black approximate balance plus generous white coloring surfaces, crisp clean contours following forms, no generic museum corridor, no classical symmetrical columns, no glowing moon, no fake reflective faces, no gray gradients, no micro-pattern wallpaper, no typography, no border.
