## Critical defects found and fixed

### Day 1 — source-function drift
The Version 02 talk prompt had shifted from “one word about how you feel now” to “one word about the day”.

**FIXED:** restored the original one-word emotional check-in as **JEDNO SŁOWO NA TERAZ** while keeping the Polish tone low-pressure.

### Day 13 — missing source mechanic
The family-map / treasure mechanic had disappeared during transcreation.

**FIXED:** restored the map frame without restoring the sentimental English wording.

### Day 21 — consent logic contradiction
The game offered a no-touch alternative, but the next instructions still required hand squeezing.

**FIXED:** the whole group now chooses either the touch or contact-free mode for the round, and both modes have complete signal mechanics.

### Day 20 — punctuation defect
A quoted question used an incorrect extra full stop after the question mark.

**FIXED.**

### Day 2 — timing joke could be read as a factual timing claim
The joke said “six seconds”, while the source requires counting to six, not measuring six seconds.

**FIXED:** joke now refers to “liczenie do sześciu”.
