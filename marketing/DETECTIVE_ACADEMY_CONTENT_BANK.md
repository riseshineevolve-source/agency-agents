# Detective Academy Content Bank — Launch Masters

Status: READY FOR PRODUCTION
Rule: each master must be adapted natively, not copied blindly between platforms.

## MASTER 01 — The Missing Detective

Hook:
**One detective is still missing.**

Visual:
black envelope -> blank Academy badge -> child's name line.

Core idea:
the reader is not watching the mystery; the reader joins it.

Parent payoff:
an activity with immediate role/agency, not a worksheet.

CTA pre-launch:
**The next case is coming.**

CTA live:
**Join the Academy.**

Formats:
- IG carousel
- 8–12 sec page-motion Reel/Short
- FB story post
- launch listing graphic

## MASTER 02 — 30 Cases, One Mystery

Hook:
**30 cases. One hidden mystery.**

Core idea:
not 30 disconnected puzzles.

Safe teaser:
**Keep your old case files. You may need them again.**

CTA:
Save this if your kid loves mysteries that get bigger as they go.

## MASTER 03 — Not Every Solved Case Is Finished

CTA:
Follow the evidence.

## MASTER 04 — The Book Is Evidence

Hook:
**What if the puzzle book itself was part of the mystery?**

CTA:

## MASTER 05 — Stuck Is Detective Work

Hook:
**Getting stuck doesn't mean the case is over.**

Core idea:
3-level Hint Vault supports progress without immediately giving away the solution.

Structure:
Nudge 1 -> notice
Nudge 2 -> focus
Nudge 3 -> stronger deduction support

CTA:
Save this for the kid who hates being told the answer.

Avoid:
claims about confidence outcomes beyond the actual product mechanic.

## MASTER 06 — Why, Not Just What

Hook:

Core idea:
solutions explain why the evidence fits.

Parent hesitation solved:
this is not just answer-checking.

CTA:
See how a real case gets closed.

## MASTER 11 — The Parent FAQ

Questions:
- What age? 8–12.
- Is it one story? 30 missions inside one book-long mystery.
- What if they get stuck? 3-level Hint Vault.
- Are solutions included? yes, reasoning-first.
- Is it screen-free? yes, pencil-friendly.
- Is it a workbook? position as case-file adventure, not worksheet.

CTA live:
View the full case file on Amazon.
