## Three approved creative worlds
### H — The House That Remembers
20 connected scenes in a sentient impossible townhouse. Toothless key, contradictory mirrors, domestic memory; one unfinished midnight welcome; climax returns to the foyer reinterpreted as an invitation rather than a trap. Adult black cat may appear sporadically but is not a repetitive mascot. Optical language: inverted domestic architecture, stair gravity, keyhole negative space, shadow doubles, reflective thresholds, folding rooms. Conflict: does house imprison guests or protect an unfinished promise? Resolution: owner of key completes welcome.

### C — The Midnight Carnival Below the City
20 connected scenes descending below an abandoned tram station into a beautiful impossible carnival. Reflected-light ticket, last show frozen by an absent beat of applause; attractions restore perception of rhythm, gravity, spotlight, echo and collective participation. Masked ringmaster is ambiguity, not stock villain. Optical language: stage depth, fabric planes, show machinery, unstable trapeze perspective, lacquered reflective pit, shadows and high-contrast theatrical motion. Protagonist moves from spectator to participant. Finale: dawn and completed show.

### M — The Eclipse Museum of Lost Nights
20 connected exhibits containing possibilities of nights never lived, not corpses or stolen memories. Glass reflections show potential futures; eclipse gap is small recurring visual counterpoint, not big circular stamp on every page. Optical language: sculptural porcelain, black glass, anamorphic silhouettes, object cases, folded velvets, fractured reflections, impossible gallery sections, negative-space shadows. Protagonist discovers museum shelters possibilities rather than consuming time, chooses to release one new night through rooftop lantern.

## Studio art direction
Premium B/W art, 8.5×11 portrait, 40–55% deep-black aim when suitable; broad clean white coloring regions; real dimensionality; no generic Halloween stock art, mandala, chibi, fuzzy fur, repeated arched halls or infinite generic swirls. Every image: one hero, one governing optic, secondary discovery, thumbnail story, clear camera and meaningful narrative contribution. Reflections should obey a traceable reflective surface; paradoxes deliberate with explicit dislocation. Avoid repeated composition in adjacent scenes.
