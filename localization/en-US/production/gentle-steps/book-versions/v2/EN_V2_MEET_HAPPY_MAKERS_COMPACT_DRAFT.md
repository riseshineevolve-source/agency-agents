## MEET THE HAPPY MAKERS

They are not a perfectly calm Christmas family.

They lose things, run late, change plans, laugh at the wrong moment, and somehow still manage to make ordinary December nights worth remembering.

### NINI, 4
**The Tiny Chaos Detector**

The youngest Happy Maker. Nini dances to almost anything with a beat, "reads" books with complete confidence, and notices household nonsense before everyone else. Small person. Very large opinions.

### ALIO, 7
**The Chief Fun Engineer**

Alio builds block cities, invents games, launches into Kung Fu with dramatic commitment, and brings enough energy for several people at once. Most plans become more interesting when he gets involved.

### DILO, 9
**The Systems Guy**

Football, games, tech, rules, timing: Dilo likes knowing how things work and whether the result counts. He brings mock-official quality control to family chaos and takes surprisingly good care of the people inside it.

### LULI, 12
**The Calm Detective**

The oldest sibling. Luli loves mysteries, aerial silks, language, and spotting the detail everyone else missed. She is precise, stylish, quietly funny, and rarely wastes a sentence.

### MIMI
**The Family Control Center**

Mimi can be thinking about dinner, tomorrow's schedule, a missing glove, and whether anyone has seen the tape at the same time. She brings practical warmth, quick decisions, and the useful reminder that family life does not need to be tidy to be good.
