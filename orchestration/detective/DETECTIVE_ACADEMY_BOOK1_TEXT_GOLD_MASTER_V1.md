## MEET THE HAPPY MAKERS

The Detective Academy does not have a “normal” day.

Some mornings begin with a missing trophy. Others involve a parrot with suspiciously good timing, a backpack with the wrong owner, or Dilo announcing that a perfectly ordinary locker has “entered competitive mode.”

That is why the case table has five field detectives around it — and why it is almost never tidy.

**Mimi** turns a muddle into the next useful step.  
**Luli** reads the small print and asks what the evidence actually proves.  
**Dilo** likes codes, machines and being right slightly before the test is finished.  
**Alio** checks routes, corners and anything that might justify carrying a helmet.  
**Nini** gives people time to speak and notices what louder theories miss.  
And **Grandma Bibi**, the Academy’s archive mentor, knows where the old files are kept — though she strongly prefers evidence to being asked whether she “remembers everything because she is old.”

They solve cases together.

Usually.

There is just one problem.

At the portable certification rack beside the intake desk, five field badges hang in a neat row.

**Hook 06 is empty.**

### MIMI — FIELD DETECTIVE // CASE CAPTAIN

**What Mimi brings to a case:** Turns a muddle into the next useful step.

- Runs the case table.
- Finds the tiny clue.
- May need help finding her own phone.

**Known habit:** Organizes everything except what she just put down.

### LULI — FIELD DETECTIVE // CLUE ANALYST

**What Luli brings to a case:** Spots contradictions and tests what evidence proves.

- Reads the small print.
- Questions the big claim.
- Short replies. Long books.

**Known habit:** Can forget a book is not a substitute for lunch.

### DILO — FIELD DETECTIVE // CODE & TECH

**What Dilo brings to a case:** Connects numbers, machines and clever experiments.

- Loves codes, games and close scores.
- Invents impressive-sounding facts.
- Will help you test them.

**Known habit:** His confidence can finish before the test does.

### ALIO — FIELD DETECTIVE // ROUTES & RECON

**What Alio brings to a case:** Notices routes and useful physical details.

- Asks the extra question.
- Checks the unexpected corner.
- Packed a helmet for this introduction.

**Known habit:** Prepares for imaginary dangers before reading ordinary signs.

### NINI — FIELD DETECTIVE // PEOPLE & COURAGE

**What Nini brings to a case:** Notices feelings and details everyone skipped.

- Gives nervous people room to speak.
- Gives serious objects cute names.
- Asks small, surprisingly good questions.

**Known habit:** Can become fond of a theory before it earns its place.

### GRANDMA BIBI — ARCHIVE MENTOR // OLD ACADEMY GUIDE

**What Grandma Bibi brings to a case:** Connects historical records and knows what to verify.

- Knows old shelves and stranger stories.
- Says when a memory is incomplete.
- Stops nonsense without putting down her tea.

**Known habit:** Remembers the adventure; the date may need a label.

## HOW TO INVESTIGATE

- Read **CASE FILE // WHAT HAPPENED**. Understand the situation before solving anything.
- Check **YOUR OBJECTIVE**. Know exactly what your verdict must identify.
- Read **INVESTIGATION RULES** before touching the puzzle.
- Read **HAPPY MAKERS CHAT** for character, context and useful thinking — never for a hidden rule.
- Study the evidence. Pictures, positions, labels, times and wording can all matter.
- Mark what you know. For map cases, record every final witness position, not only the requested answer.
- Use the response surface made for that puzzle: circle, sort, mark, order, code or write only what the task actually requires.
- Read **CASE CLOSED** after solving so you know what the local result means.
- If a case says **KEEP THIS**, keep it. An old case may become evidence again.
- Stuck? Use one Hint Vault level at a time. A hint is a nudge, not a verdict.
