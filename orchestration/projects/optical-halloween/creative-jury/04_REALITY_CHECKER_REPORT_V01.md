## 5. Construction tests, and what has actually been checked

### Reflection accountability

For a unit mirror normal `n` and plane `n·X = c`, the virtual source point is `P' = P − 2n(n·P − c)`. The line from eye `E` to `P'` must meet the **finite reflective area**, and the two actual ray segments must remain unobstructed. A consistent projection is required for each source contour, not just one highlighted point. A plane mirror reverses the coordinate normal to its plane; “left/right flip” is not an adequate construction instruction. The anatomical left hand remains the same hand in transformed coordinates.

Two numerical examples were calculated in the tool runtime for this report. They prove only local point visibility, not the layout of any proposed or generated image:

- **H-01 local feasibility example, metres:** mirror `x = 0`; eye `E = (0.65, −0.20, 1.55)`; key witness `P = (0.18, 0.35, 1.25)`; latch witness `L = (0.20, 0.37, 1.25)`. Their reflected points are `(-0.18, 0.35, 1.25)` and `(-0.20, 0.37, 1.25)`. Eye rays meet the mirror at approximately `(0, 0.2307, 1.3151)` and `(0, 0.2359, 1.3206)`. Both fit an example mirror spanning `y = −0.10..0.60`, `z = 1.10..1.70`. This shows that one plane can admit both witnesses. Jamb occlusion, the full objects, hand clearance, camera projection, and actual mirror mounting still need layout proof.
- **H-02 local footprint example, metres:** floor `z = 0`; relief witness `(0, 0, 2.40)`; eye `(4, 0, 0.12)`; virtual witness `(0, 0, −2.40)`. The eye ray meets the floor at `(3.8095, 0, 0)`, near the observer. A very low camera does not authorize placing the reflected lintel arbitrarily halfway across the room. Its finite reflection patch must include that intersection. This is not a complete foyer or cat model.

## 10. Concrete rejection rules for the recovery jury

1. **Optical impact below 9/10:** reject the layout even if its props are attractive, its weighted score looks favorable, or it follows the plot. Test the main spatial effect at thumbnail and the explanatory second reading at intended size.
2. **Same curved corridor repeated:** reject three scenes built from the same tunnel, centered swirl, or camera, even if the props change. Bands belong to the specified fitting and its load or material logic.
3. **Decorative striping only:** reject a conventional drawer, roof, bed, or stove with surface waves that neither define depth nor drive its optical reading. Thick curved lines must articulate actual physical form.
4. **Unaccountable reflection:** reject changed hand identity, invented teeth, duplicate people, detached animal heads, a floor image beyond its finite patch, or reflected action from another time. HOUSE has no future-glass rule.
5. **Wrong projection or transmission:** reject ribs behind opaque fabric casting separate shadows, an arbitrary reversed line painted in water, or a cylinder behaving as a plane mirror.
6. **Anatomy or contact concealed by density:** reject an extra digit, impossible wrist, floating cup/slate/rook, unsupported bed/piano structure, or cat weight missing its floor. Simplify bands before sacrificing anatomy.
7. **Unbounded fantasy:** reject simultaneous folds, gravity rotations, miniature rooms inside props, autonomous shadows, or an unexplained new mirror domain. One active local manifestation is enough.
8. **Stock Halloween substitution:** reject pumpkins, skull keys/cups, skeletal chess, ghost bedding, cathedral ornament, Victorian lace, pet hero art, and sentimental welcoming faces. The midnight interruption supplies the story's unease.
9. **Unusable white regions:** reject fine channels masquerading as generous coloring space, gray volume, tiny lacework, or filled black masses that erase working contact. Flexible black coverage is subordinate to readability.
10. **False certification:** reject any declaration that an ungenerated concept is GOLD, optically proven, owner-approved, or print-ready. Actual evidence must precede clearance.
