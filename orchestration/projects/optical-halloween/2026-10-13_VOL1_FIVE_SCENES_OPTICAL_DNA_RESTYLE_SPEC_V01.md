## Mandatory Master Style
- Pure solid ink #000000 and clean paper #FFFFFF only, zero gray/halftone/soft shading/crosshatching.
- Physical line weights at 8.5x11in trim: dominant hero outlines 1.6–2.6mm, primary architecture 1.1–1.8mm, support details 0.6–1.0mm, broad black optical bands ~4–25mm. Distinct weight hierarchy, NOT uniformly thin outlines.
- Make the NEW candidate significantly MORE COLORABLE: target 35–50% solid black after high-resolution binary production; wide enclosed white hero and architecture regions roughly 15–50mm on page and most smaller regions >8–12mm. No predominantly black suit or already blacked-in room. Hero ~45–65% frame; wardrobe ~65–75%.
- 1 optical paradox dominates each artwork and is traceable on second look. Camera angle is physically coherent across ceiling, walls, props and gravity; no fake tilt achieved by merely turning stairs.
- Prohibit repeated Gothic cathedrals, long spiral staircase tunnels except the specific accepted hotel fall, identical portals/reflecting floors, clocks, moon/star motifs, random suitcases, signage/text, skeleton duplication, mask dolls, micro-detail.
- Vertical single artworks 8.5x11in (1:1.294 width:height), no decorative sheets/contact layouts. Real print master >=2550x3300px/300dpi; generated smaller proof is NOT falsely labelled print gold.
- No image approved as GOLD until viewed side-by-side with actual 14 selected and passes colorability, distortion/anatomy and no-repeat review.
