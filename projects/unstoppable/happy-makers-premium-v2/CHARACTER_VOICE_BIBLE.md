# Character Voice Bible — Happy Makers / Project Unstoppable V2

Canonical roster: exactly six.

## Mimi — Case Captain
Function: turns muddles into useful next steps; holds the group together without acting like a teacher.
Voice: concise, capable, warm, dry authority. Good at saying "we are solving the actual problem, not the dramatic version of it."
Humor: deadpan shutdown of overcomplication, especially Dilo's operation names and Alio's unnecessary risk planning.
Never: bossy coach, perfect older-sister archetype, motivational speaker.

## Luli — Clue Analyst
Function: tests claims, asks what evidence actually proves, catches overstatement and fuzzy thinking.
Voice: sharp, curious, observant, quietly funny.
Humor: skeptical one-liners, calling out assumptions, precise social observations.
Never: cynical, cold, pedantic, therapy voice.

## Dilo — Code & Tech
Function: codes, systems, timers, tools, experiments, practical shortcuts.
Voice: fast, inventive, mildly dramatic, loves naming operations and building systems.
Humor: over-engineers harmless things; the others affectionately deflate him.
Never: endless gamer slang, tech-bro, fake teenage internet voice, mockery of vulnerable moments.

## Alio — Routes & Recon
Function: movement, routes, risk checks, practical environment design, getting from A to B.
Voice: physical, direct, action-first, slightly adventurous.
Humor: suspicious enthusiasm for routes, corners, gear and helmet-level precautions.
Never: reckless daredevil, macho, safety used as a joke.

## Nini — People & Courage
Function: notices quiet details, social dynamics, courage, boundaries, kindness and inclusion.
Voice: warm, perceptive, unexpectedly bold; says the thing everyone else missed.
Humor: gentle but incisive; can land the shortest line in the room.
Never: babyish, overly sweet, emotional mascot, forced empathy.

## Grandma Bibi — Archive Mentor
Function: context, memory, verified facts, experience, long view, good questions.
Voice: spare, calm, exact, funny because she refuses nonsense.
Humor: dry understatement; occasionally one devastating word ("No.") after a long team debate.
Never: mystical grandma, lecture machine, old-fashioned caricature.

## Team dynamic
They are affectionate, mildly snarky, cooperative and useful.
They disagree without humiliating one another.
They know each other's habits.
They support the reader like a teammate, not a client/student.
No one character is always right.
The reader gradually moves from observer to active seventh seat at the table without creating a new named Happy Maker.

## Humor standard
- joke at the situation, never at a vulnerable answer;
- one strong exchange beats five filler jokes;
- humor must reveal relationship or character;
- no meme archaeology;
- no fake youth slang;
- no joke attached to bullying/threat disclosure, consent, fear, worthlessness or safety escalation.

## Book-wide progression of relationship
Days 1–7: reader meets all six and learns how they work.
Days 8–14: reader starts answering back / testing tools alongside them.
Days 15–21: Happy Makers treat reader as someone whose judgment matters.
Days 22–27: reader makes independent choices and brings evidence to the team.
Days 28–31: reader is explicitly treated as part of the crew; final message is invitation/continuation, not graduation from the Happy Makers.
