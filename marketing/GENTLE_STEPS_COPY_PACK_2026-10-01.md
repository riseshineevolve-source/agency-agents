## Prelaunch captions

### Caption 01 — Ten minutes
December is already full.

School. Work. Gifts. Lists. The mysteriously missing sock that apparently has holiday plans of its own.

So this is not another big Christmas project.

24 Gentle Steps to Christmas is built around one tiny daily ritual: about ten minutes, no prep, no extra materials, no cleanup.

A Mindful Moment.
A Fun Spark.
A Connection Share.

That is it.

One small chance to pause, laugh and actually notice each other before the day disappears.

Save this for December.

### Caption 02 — What happens each day
What can ten minutes actually look like?

One minute to pause.
A quick playful family activity.
One short moment to share something real.

That is the daily rhythm behind 24 Gentle Steps to Christmas.

Not a perfect-family challenge.
Not a craft marathon.
Not another December task list.

Just one small ritual designed to fit the family you already are.

### Caption 03 — Sparkling tornado
If December feels like a beautiful, sparkling tornado, this is your soft landing.

Not silence.
Not perfection.
Not a perfectly styled family around a perfectly styled tree.

Just ten minutes where everyone stops being a logistics department for a moment and becomes a family again.

### Caption 04 — Calm can be fun
Calm does not have to mean sitting perfectly still while everyone whispers.

In this book, a quiet minute can be followed by a ridiculous mini-game, a funny Happy Makers note and one honest sentence from each person.

Because family connection can be warm, playful and slightly chaotic at the same time.

### Caption 05 — Why no prep matters
“No prep” is not a small feature in December.

It means no shopping list.
No craft supplies.
No setup.
No cleanup.
No parent quietly regretting the tradition they started on December 3rd.

Open the day.
Do the three tiny moments.
Move on with your evening.

That is the point.

### Caption 06 — Memory
The goal is not to do more this Christmas.

It is to notice more of what is already here.

The joke someone keeps repeating.
The answer you did not expect.
The child who suddenly says something honest.
The ten quiet minutes that become the thing you remember in March.

24 days. Small moments. One December you actually felt.
