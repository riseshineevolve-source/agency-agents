## Why the split is justified

The product-intent SERP is dominated by purchasable mystery books with explicit case counts, difficulty and solution features.

The screen-free-activity SERP includes:
- physical kits;
- printable mysteries;
- family activities;
- activity-specific landing pages;
- time/use-case framing.

That is a different decision state:
- product intent asks "which book/product?";
- activity intent asks "what can my child do offline that feels like a mystery?".

A dedicated activity/sampler surface can answer that second question better without diluting the core product page.
