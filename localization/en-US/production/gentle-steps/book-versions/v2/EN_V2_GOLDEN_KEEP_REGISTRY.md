# Gentle Steps EN V2 — GOLDEN KEEP Registry

Status: ACTIVE

## Product-level locks inherited from PL R4

1. **24 days / 3-part daily architecture**
   - PAUSE
   - PLAY
   - BETWEEN US

2. **Every PAUSE gets a practical TRY IT TOMORROW transfer cue.**

3. **No broad return to "mindfulness" branding.**
   The old English "Mindful Moment" system is superseded.

4. **Game portfolio diversity**
   Preserve the curated mix of:
   - team/cooperation;
   - genuine laughter;
   - word/improv;
   - other best-of.

5. **Every game has**
   - clear start;
   - play loop;
   - ending/finale;
   - two-person version;
   - group-size/safety clarification where needed.

6. **Day 1 Invisible Ball is a hero concept.**
   It opens the book and returns on Day 24.

7. **Day 9 is PLAN B: 60 SECONDS.**
   Old English Human Knot is rejected/superseded.

8. **Day 17 ONLY QUESTIONS is a hero game.**

9. **Day 18 Chair Mission / navigation mechanic**
   Preserve:
   - short safe route;
   - both people see route first;
   - open eyes are a full version;
   - no speed points;
   - explicit odd-group rotation.

10. **Day 19 Empty Hands**
    Preserve function and the domestic joke calibration.

11. **Day 20 silent-line team game**
    Preserve nonverbal family teamwork and eyebrow humor.

12. **Day 23 Luckily / Unfortunately**
    Preserve as fictional improv directly before optional real apology.
    Do not reintroduce "alibi" framing.

13. **Day 23 apology**
    - optional;
    - specific;
    - no "but you...";
    - listener does not owe immediate forgiveness or "it's okay".

14. **Day 24 Four Minutes. Four Missions.**
    Must remain the core finale.
    Singing is optional encore only.
    Invisible Ball returns as final callback.

15. **Final family conversation**
    Keep:
    - one thing worth repeating;
    - one wish.

16. **Back matter function**
    Keep:
    - one later evening;
    - no "new family program";
    - take only what actually worked;
    - ordinary-life reuse of tools;
    - warm Happy Makers letter.
